## What keeps recurring
- This is the second confirmed instance (after the Aug 9 session's search-synthesis citation issue) of trusting a written claim without checking its actual source. Worth treating "verify before citing as fact" as a standing default for ANY citation — internal doc, skill file, or external search — not just external search results specifically.
- Multiple sessions now involve a pattern of: build something with an assumption baked in → real production surfaces a failure → the fix over-corrects → a later session has to re-diagnose and un-overcorrect. The `POV_LOCK_CLAUSE` history (added to fix one thing, ended up causing a different visible problem) is a clean example. Worth considering, next time a "confirmed failure" fix gets written into a locked skill/checklist, stating explicitly what the fix should NOT also do (the over-correction boundary), not just what it should fix.
