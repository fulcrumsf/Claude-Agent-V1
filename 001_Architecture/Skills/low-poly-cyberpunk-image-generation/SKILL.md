# Low Poly Cyberpunk Image Generation

## Purpose
Generate reusable image prompts for **Nano Banana** and **OpenAI ChatGPT Image Generation** that produce the custom **Low Poly Cyberpunk** look.

This skill turns any visual idea into a cinematic, low-poly, neon, analog-textured image with clean storytelling and strong visual hierarchy.

## Style Summary
Low Poly Cyberpunk is a hybrid of:

- Low-poly 3D video-game character design
- Neon cyberpunk lighting
- Analog paper grain
- Origami-like geometric surfaces
- Dark cinematic contrast
- Editorial YouTube thumbnail composition
- Subtle grunge and tactile texture

The result should feel like a futuristic documentary thumbnail made from angular folded paper, neon light, and cinematic 3D objects.

## Core Visual Traits
Use these in every prompt:

- Angular low-poly 3D forms
- Faceted characters and objects
- Subtle rice-paper texture on surfaces
- Neon cyan, teal, magenta, and occasional lime/yellow highlights
- Dark navy, black, or deep teal background
- Cinematic rim lighting
- Shallow depth and strong focal point
- Slight analog grain, dust, scratches, halftone, or paper fibers
- Clean symbolic storytelling

## Prompt Formula
Use this structure for any image request:

```text
Create a [aspect ratio] image in a Low Poly Cyberpunk style.
Subject: [main subject].
Story idea: [what the image should imply].
Composition: one clear hero subject, one symbolic supporting element, minimal background detail.
Style: angular low-poly 3D geometry, faceted origami-like surfaces, subtle rice-paper grain, dark cinematic background, neon cyan and magenta rim lighting, selective lime/yellow glow accents, analog grunge texture, premium editorial look.
Mood: [mysterious / dramatic / clever / tense / futuristic / documentary].
Avoid clutter, long text, excessive props, photorealistic skin, flat vector art, and busy infographic labels.
```

## OpenAI ChatGPT Image Generation Prompt Template
Use this when prompting ChatGPT image generation:

```text
Create a [9:16 vertical / 16:9 horizontal / 1:1 square] image in a custom Low Poly Cyberpunk style.

The image should show [main subject] and visually suggest [core idea] using only one simple symbolic clue: [symbolic clue].

The subject should look like an angular low-poly 3D video-game character/object, with faceted planes, subtle folded-paper geometry, and a refined origami-like rice-paper texture. Use a dark cinematic background with neon cyan and magenta rim lighting, small lime/yellow accent glow, subtle analog grain, dust, and paper fibers.

Keep the composition simple, bold, and readable. Do not overcrowd the image. Avoid long text, excessive arrows, small labels, photorealistic portrait styling, and busy infographic elements.
```

## Optional Text Rules
Only include text when it improves clickability.

Text rules:

- 0–4 words maximum
- Large, bold, simple
- High contrast
- Never explain the whole story
- Avoid subtitles and small captions

Good examples:

- `$700?!`
- `FAILED?`
- `TOO EXPENSIVE`
- `BEFORE APPLE`
- `NO HOTELS`

Bad examples:

- `How Warby Parker disrupted the eyewear industry`
- `The story of how two founders solved expensive glasses`
- `This company became worth billions after a strange pivot`

## Lighting Recipe
Use this lighting structure:

- Key light: soft teal/cyan from front-left
- Rim light: magenta or pink from back-right
- Accent light: neon lime/yellow on the symbolic object
- Background: dark blue/black gradient with subtle texture
- Surface texture: paper grain visible but not distracting

## Texture Recipe
Add subtle texture language:

- rice-paper grain
- folded paper fibers
- analog dust
- halftone speckles
- worn poster texture
- light scratches
- matte finish

Avoid making it look dirty or overly distressed. The texture should feel premium and intentional.

## Composition Recipes

### Profile Character Thumbnail
```text
One low-poly character in dramatic side profile, large in frame, looking toward a symbolic object in the background. Dark background, neon rim light, subtle paper texture, no headline text.
```

### Product Mystery Thumbnail
```text
One product/object centered large in frame, glowing edge light, one contradictory clue beside it, simple dark background, minimal text or none.
```

### Before/After Visual
```text
Split composition with one old object on the left and one modern object on the right, connected by a single neon arrow. Keep both objects large and simple.
```

### Failure / Scam / Problem Visual
```text
One frustrated low-poly character, one oversized price tag or red X symbol, dark background, neon accent, minimal props.
```

```text
Show a broke grad student in side profile wearing glasses and holding a backpack strap. Behind him is one torn-paper receipt that says "$700" with a red X over it, plus a faint Southeast Asia map silhouette.

The subject should look like an angular low-poly 3D video-game character, with faceted planes, subtle folded-paper geometry, and refined rice-paper texture. Use dark teal shadows, cyan rim light, magenta edge glow, subtle analog grain, and premium editorial composition.

```

### Shopify
```text

Show one small snowy snowboard shop on the left and one glowing green shopping-bag platform icon on the right, connected by a single neon arrow.

Use angular low-poly 3D forms, dark cinematic background, cyan and green rim lighting, subtle paper grain, and clean visual hierarchy. Keep the scene simple and avoid explaining the entire story.

```

```text
Show one glossy green shopping bag with a recycle symbol peeling open to reveal dark smoke and discarded clothing underneath.

Use faceted low-poly geometry, origami-like paper texture, dark cinematic shadows, neon green glow, subtle magenta edge light, and analog paper grain. Make the idea readable visually without relying on text.

```

```text
cluttered layout, too many objects, long text, small labels, infographic callouts, excessive arrows, photorealistic portrait, smooth plastic skin, flat vector art, cartoon mascot, generic stock photo, chaotic background, unreadable mobile thumbnail, too much detail, messy typography, oversaturated colors
```

## Final Quality Test
The generated image should pass these checks:

- One idea is visible instantly.
- The hero subject dominates the frame.
- The story is suggested, not explained.
- The texture is subtle and premium.
- The neon lighting supports the focal point.
- It still works when viewed small on a phone.
