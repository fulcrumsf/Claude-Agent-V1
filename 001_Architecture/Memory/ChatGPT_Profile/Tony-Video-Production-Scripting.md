## Prompt Style Signals
- Prompts are often dense, layered, and packed with constraints.
- Tony frequently trims, simplifies, or removes unwanted elements.
- He often wants several variants instead of one final answer.
- He likes structured breakdowns and repeatable frameworks.
- Visual direction, layout, and production constraints matter as much as the idea itself.

## Key Preferences & Opinions

- Wants scripts and beats that are easy to hand off into editing or Remotion.
