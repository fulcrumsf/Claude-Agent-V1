## Correction: `isBrandedContent` is not the same as affiliate/commission content

Proposed setting Blotato's `isBrandedContent: true` for the Colorsmart Pens TikTok
post, reasoning "it's paid promotional content." Tony corrected this directly:
`isBrandedContent` is specifically for a direct brand-paid partnership where the
brand dictates content guidelines — a true "sponsored post." TikTok Shop
Creator/affiliate content (commission on sales, GMV-tied fees, no brand paying for
this specific video or directing its content) is a different arrangement entirely.

**Why it matters:** These are legally/contractually distinct relationships on
TikTok's platform, and conflating them would mis-tag every future Shop Creator
post. **How to apply:** `isBrandedContent` should be `false` for any TikTok Shop
Creator/affiliate video in this pipeline going forward — it is never the right
flag for commission-based affiliate content, regardless of how "promotional" the
content is.
