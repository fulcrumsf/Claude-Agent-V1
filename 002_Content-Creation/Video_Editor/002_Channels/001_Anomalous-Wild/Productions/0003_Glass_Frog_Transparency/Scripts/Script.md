SCENE 03 — TEASE #1 — 27–87s (60s)
---
NARRATION:
"Through that skin, you can see its heart, beating in real time."
"Its liver. Its lungs. The coiled loops of its intestines."
"No dissection. No X-ray. Just daylight passing through living tissue."
"Seeing the heart this clearly is rare, even among glass frogs — some species only show you bone, or the outline of the gut."
"But here's what should be impossible: none of those organs look like a dark red blob under the skin."
"They're tucked inside tiny mirrored pouches, lined with reflective crystal, that bounce light around them instead of absorbing it."
"The frog isn't just transparent. It's actively camouflaging its own insides."
"And that's still not the most anomalous part."

VISUAL:
1. [0–5s] AI-generated 3D render, POV/internal: camera pushes in on the beating heart visible through skin, synced pulse.
2. [5–10s] AI-generated 3D render: cutaway showing liver, lungs, and intestinal loops in sequence, labeled forms only (no text baked into the image itself — labels added later in Remotion).
3. [10–14s] Macro, real footage style: full ventral view, organs visible, calm and still.
4. [14–18s] Species comparison: quick 3-frame cut across different glass frog species showing varying degrees of transparency (bone-only vs. full organ visibility).
5. [18–23s] Zoom punch on a single organ silhouette — normal dark-red expectation vs. reality.
6. [23–28s] AI-generated 3D render, extreme macro: cutaway of the mirrored pouch structure, crystal-like guanine surface reflecting light around the organ.
7. [28–33s] Slow push out from the mirrored pouch to the whole visible torso — organs present, but no dark shapes reading through.
8. [33–38s] Deliberate 0.5–1s beat of near-silence, held wide shot of the frog motionless on a leaf, before the tease line.
SOUND: Ambient forest lowers to near-silence before "And that's still not the most anomalous part," then a subtle rising tone/stinger.
ON-SCREEN TEXT: Fact Callout — "Mirrored organ pouches" (fly in synced to that line)

SCENE 04 — CONTEXT LOOP — 87–117s (30s)
---
NARRATION:
"There are around a hundred and fifty species of glass frog, spread from southern Mexico down through the Andes and into the Amazon basin."
"They live in the trees overhanging mountain streams, in cloud forests thick with mist."
"By night, they're ambush hunters — sitting motionless, then snapping up insects with a single flick of the tongue."
"By day, they sleep hanging beneath a leaf, pressed flat, pale and nearly shadowless."
"Seen from the side, against that leaf, there's almost nothing to see at all."

VISUAL:

2. [5–9s] Wide, overhead/God view: cloud forest canopy over a mountain stream, mist visible.

4. [13–17s] Macro, real footage style: frog motionless on a leaf underside by daylight.
5. [17–24s] Wide shot from below/side: frog pressed flat against a backlit leaf, edges nearly disappearing into the leaf's own translucency.
6. [24–30s] Slow zoom out to full leaf canopy — frog nearly imperceptible in the wide frame.
SOUND: Ambient shifts from night forest (insects, distant frog calls) to daytime forest quiet (birdsong, wind through leaves) as narration crosses the day/night line.
ON-SCREEN TEXT: Location Card — "Cloud Forests, Central & South America" (hold 2–3s, fade)

SCENE 05 — TEASE #2 — 117–187s (70s)
---
NARRATION:
"Science has only recently discovered how far this trick actually goes."
"In 2022, researchers at the American Museum of Natural History found that glass frogs don't just have transparent skin — they can turn their own transparency up on command."
"While a glass frog sleeps, it pulls nearly ninety percent of its red blood cells out of general circulation."
"It packs them into one organ. Its liver."
"With that much of its blood cells removed from the rest of its body, the frog becomes two to three times more transparent than when it's awake."
"We'll come back to why that matters — but first, consider what should happen next."
"In almost every other vertebrate on Earth, concentrating blood cells that densely triggers dangerous clotting."
"Glass frogs do this every single day, and their blood never clots at all."
"Researchers only caught this happening using a technique called photoacoustic imaging — pulses of light that make blood cells vibrate audibly, mapped without a single incision, on a frog that never even woke up."

VISUAL:
1. [0–4s] Wide shot: frog asleep on a leaf, calm, undisturbed.
2. [4–8s] AI-generated 3D render, internal: red blood cells shown flowing through vessels at normal density (awake state) — stylized, not graphic.
3. [8–13s] AI-generated 3D render: the same cells shown draining out of general circulation, concentrating toward the liver.
4. [13–18s] AI-generated 3D render, macro: liver organ visibly filling, mirrored pouch reflecting light around the concentrated mass so it stays camouflaged.
5. [18–23s] Side-by-side comparison shot: awake frog (more visible) vs. sleeping frog (near-invisible), same leaf, same angle.
6. [23–28s] Deliberate near-silence beat, wide shot held on the sleeping frog — tease-line pause before the escalation.
7. [28–33s] AI-generated 3D render: stylized vessel cross-section, dense cell concentration, tension building (no gore — clean scientific-diagram aesthetic, not viscera).
8. [33–38s] Cut to contrast: a stylized human vascular cross-section for one beat, showing what this concentration would normally cause, before cutting away.
9. [38–43s] Return to frog — calm, unaffected, sleeping peacefully.
10. [43–48s] Lab/equipment insert (AI-generated or stock lab visual): photoacoustic imaging device, pulses of light visualized as sound-wave ripples over the frog's body, frog undisturbed on its perch.
SOUND: Score swells beginning at "In 2022, researchers..."; brief silence beat before "consider what should happen next"; stinger on "their blood never clots at all."
ON-SCREEN TEXT: Fact Callout — "90% of red blood cells hidden" and "2–3x more transparent," each synced to its line; Anomaly Level Meter fill to 9/10 during the clotting-paradox line.
TRANSITION: Glitch cut into visual 2; hard cuts elsewhere; slow-mo freeze on visual 5's comparison

SCENE 06 — REWARD — 187–227s (40s)
---
NARRATION:
"That contradiction is exactly what makes this discovery matter far beyond one frog in one rainforest."
"In humans, the balance between clotting and bleeding is one of the hardest problems in medicine — too much clotting causes strokes and heart attacks; too little causes uncontrolled bleeding."
"One of the researchers called solving that balance the holy grail of hematology."
"A glass frog solves it daily, without trying, every time it falls asleep."
"What we now know is that studying this small, transparent animal could shape how we treat blood clots in humans — turning a rainforest camouflage trick into a lead for cardiovascular research."
"Of all the creatures on Earth working on this problem, the answer may already be sleeping under a leaf, upside down, invisible."

VISUAL:
1. [0–5s] Wide, cinematic: frog on its leaf, forest around it, camera slowly pulling back.
2. [5–10s] AI-generated 3D render: abstract human circulatory system visual, clean scientific-infographic style, illustrating the clotting/bleeding balance.
3. [10–14s] Cut to researcher/lab-context insert (AI-generated or stock, non-identifiable), photoacoustic imaging visualization overlay.
4. [14–19s] Return to frog, calm and sleeping, held shot.
5. [19–27s] Slow push in on the frog's transparent belly one final time, heart still visible, organs at rest.
6. [27–33s] Overhead/God view pulling back from the leaf into the wider cloud-forest canopy, night falling into day.
7. [33–40s] Final wide hold: the frog nearly vanishing into the leaf's own light as the frame fades toward the next scene.
SOUND: Score peaks under "holy grail of hematology," then settles into a calmer resolving motif through the final lines.
ON-SCREEN TEXT: Fact Callout — "Studied for human blood-clot research" (fly in, hold through end of scene)
TRANSITION: Hard cuts; slow push (no dissolve) into final scene
