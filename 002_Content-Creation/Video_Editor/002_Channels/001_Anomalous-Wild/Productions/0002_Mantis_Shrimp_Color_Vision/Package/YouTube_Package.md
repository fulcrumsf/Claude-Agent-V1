# Description

Human eyes build every color you've ever seen from just three types of receptor. The mantis shrimp carries up to sixteen — and its eyes don't stop at color. They can detect polarized light, a sense humans don't have at all.

Scientists still don't fully understand why an animal needs this much visual information, or how its brain processes it. This is the strange, still-unsolved biology of the most complex eyes in the animal kingdom.
