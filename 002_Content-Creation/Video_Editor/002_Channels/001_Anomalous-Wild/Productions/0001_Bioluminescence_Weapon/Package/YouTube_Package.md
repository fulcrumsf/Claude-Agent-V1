# Description

In the deepest, darkest part of the ocean — where sunlight has never once reached — something is glowing. And it isn't just glowing to be seen. It's hunting.

Roughly 90% of everything living below 1,000 metres produces its own light through a chemical reaction more efficient than any light source humans have ever built. For the anglerfish, that light became a weapon: a bioluminescent lure powered by living bacteria, engineered by millions of years of evolution to end in a kill.

And the strangest part? This same trick — making light from nothing but chemistry — evolved independently more than 40 separate times across completely unrelated branches of life.

This is the story of the deep ocean's most alien technology, and why nature keeps inventing it again and again.
