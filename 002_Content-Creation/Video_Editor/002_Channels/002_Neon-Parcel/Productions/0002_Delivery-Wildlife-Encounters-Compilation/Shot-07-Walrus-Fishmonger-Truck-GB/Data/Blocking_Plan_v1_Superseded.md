# Blocking Plan

**Device type: fixed/mounted static shopfront CCTV.** Nobody operates it; nothing pans, tilts or zooms. Unlike prior static-CCTV shots, the SUBJECT here includes a moving vehicle entering frame, so its full path must be planned (see Data/Site_Plan.md).

## Fixed frame
Camera mounted above/beside the fishmonger's shop door, moderate height, looking out over the street and curb, static, midday overcast light, damp stone and pavement, no current rain.
- **Background/left:** the stone shopfront facade and door.
- **Foreground/street:** the narrow village street and curb where the van parks, running left-right across the frame.
- **Right edge:** where the van enters from off-frame.

## Vehicle entry path (per the Site Plan)
The van enters from frame-right, driving left along the street, then turns right (a shallow reverse-park turn) to bring the van parallel to the curb with its REAR facing the camera square-on, stopping directly in front of the shop. Engine off; it never moves again. This is a genuine drive-and-park maneuver, not a jump-cut to "already parked."

## Subject origins (nothing appears from nowhere)
- **Van:** enters from off-frame right at frame 1, following the path above.
- **Fishmonger:** inside the van's cab during the drive-in; gets out only after it's parked.
- **Walrus:** already inside the closed cargo box from frame 1 (stowed away earlier, off-screen, at the harbor dock — not shown in this shot). It only becomes visible once the fishmonger opens the rear roll-up door.

## Beats (one continuous uncut shot)
1. Van drives in from frame-right, turns, parks with its rear door facing the camera in front of the shop.
2. Fishmonger gets out of the cab, walks to the rear of the van.
3. He rolls/lifts open the rear roller shutter door.
4. He sees the walrus, startles, half-steps back with a hand raised.
5. The walrus burps once, then waddles/heaves itself down and out of the cargo box, dragging ice and fish remains over the closed, stowed lift gate.
6. The walrus moves off toward the harbor/water direction (off-frame); the fishmonger is left standing by the open van, ice and fish heads scattered on the ground. No blood visible anywhere.

## Grounding carried into every prompt (from Research/Plausibility_Facts.md)
Walrus: subadult, ~2.0-2.3 m, grey-brown wrinkled skin, dense white whisker pad, small just-emerging tusks, no external ears, rolling/heaving waddle gait. Van: kei-truck-proportioned cab, UK-scale refrigerated box body, roll-up rear shutter door, hydraulic tail lift folded/stowed (never deployed). Ice + fish heads only, explicitly no blood. Fishmonger: waterproof bib apron/oilskin, rubber boots, no company branding needed.

## Tone / capture / audio
Unplanned real-life shopfront CCTV footage, ordinary and observational. Audio: natural location only — the van's engine and door, the roller shutter's mechanical rattle, the walrus's burp and grunts, its ice/gravelly scraping sounds, the fishmonger's startled exclamation (no intelligible words), gulls distantly. No dialogue, music or voiceover.

## Hard constraints
Exactly one fishmonger, one walrus, one van; no other people or animals. The van's path is continuous (enters, turns, parks) with no teleporting. The lift gate stays folded/closed throughout — it is never shown deployed or lowered. No blood anywhere, only ice and fish-head remains. No camera movement, no on-screen text or logos.
