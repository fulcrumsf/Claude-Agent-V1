# Concept filters — Shot 06

## What changed from the picked cell, and why (Grounding Audit finding, for Tony)
Picked hook: "a capybara settles on his motorbike seat and he politely waits." **Not physically plausible:** a Honda CG-class seat is ~0.78 m high; an adult capybara is ~0.5-0.6 m at the shoulder, cannot jump or climb onto it. Also, the research (Research/Plausibility_Facts.md) shows condo deliveries in Brazil usually stop at the portaria (the courier waits outside, removes his helmet for the ID cameras) and do not ride in. **Adapted hook, same spirit:** the capybara lies down on the one dry patch under the guardhouse awning; the motoboy politely steps out into the drizzle and waits. It uses the drizzle weather axis as the reason for the gag.

## Remarkable-but-Believable Concept Filter
- Single visual anomaly that stops the scroll: a large calm capybara settling on the dry slab beside a courier who waits in the rain.
- Why this camera captures it: a fixed guardhouse CCTV records the delivery bay 24/7; condos post such clips.
- Ordinary situation that makes it understandable: a food courier waiting at the service window for the porteiro to call the resident; it is drizzling; there is one dry spot.
- Natural escalation/consequence: the courier gives up the dry spot rather than disturb the animal.
- Cleanest ending: he puts his helmet back on for the rain and waits; the capybara closes its eyes. No return trip, no dialogue needed.

## Physical-Action Risk Filter
No fastening, handoff, catching or multi-step object manipulation. Actions: stand, look, step aside, put a helmet on. The capybara: graze, walk, lie down. Rejected as unnecessary: handing over the order, capybara touching the bag or bike, gate/barrier automation, a third person.

## Editorial check
Humor from character attitude (a sincere, polite courier) + a real behavior (capybaras rest on warm/dry ground and tolerate people). No forced joke, no stacked surprises.
