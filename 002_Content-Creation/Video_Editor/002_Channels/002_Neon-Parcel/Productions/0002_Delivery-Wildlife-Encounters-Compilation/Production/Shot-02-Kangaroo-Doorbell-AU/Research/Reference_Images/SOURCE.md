# Reference Images — Source and Usage Notes

Searched via Openverse (CC-licensed image search) for real regional-
Australian house references, per the Architectural Plausibility Check's
Production-Research-Agent requirement. Grounding only — never composited
into any final output.

- **Queenslander_Front_Steps_01.jpg** — real historic photo of a
  Queenslander-style house (Flickr, via Openverse). **Most useful image of
  the three**: confirms a raised weatherboard floor on stumps with a
  modest, short front stair run (well under 10 steps) leading to a side
  porch landing — directly grounds the corrected step-count fact in
  `Data/Architectural_Site_Plan.md`. Source:
  https://live.staticflickr.com/7280/7439565892_2a99186551_b.jpg
- **Queenslander_Railway_Complex_02.jpg** — real Queensland railway
  building photo. Confirms general regional-QLD corrugated-roof/weatherboard
  construction style but shows a commercial/rail structure, not a
  residential entry — not used for step-count or walkway grounding.
- **AU_House_Front_Porch_03.jpg** — real regional stone building. Ground-
  level entry, no raised floor or stairs — not useful for this shot's
  raised-entry grounding need. Kept for record, not used.

**Honest limitation:** none of the three shows the specific detail this
shot needs most (a clear view of a walkway continuously connecting a
driveway to a front staircase). That detail remains grounded in Tony's
directly pasted real-house examples (described in
`Data/Architectural_Site_Plan.md`) rather than an Openverse search result.
