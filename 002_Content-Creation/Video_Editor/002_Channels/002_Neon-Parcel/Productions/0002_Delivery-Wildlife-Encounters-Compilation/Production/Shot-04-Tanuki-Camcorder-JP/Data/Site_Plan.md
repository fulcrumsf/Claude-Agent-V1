# Site Plan — Rural Honshu Village Road (text form, before any image)

**Real route, described as a person/vehicle would actually travel it:**
the postal scooter travels along the narrow paved village road, stops
briefly at the filmer's property gate, then continues on. The tanuki
enters from the wooded hillside opposite the property, crosses partway
into the road's shoulder, then returns to the hillside — a single
continuous, physically walkable path.

## Plausibility check against this written plan

- **Continuous walkable path:** yes — hillside vegetation → hillside edge
  → road shoulder is one continuous route for the tanuki; gate → road →
  departure is one continuous route for the scooter. No gaps or jumps.
- **No blocked path:** nothing sits between the tanuki's entry point and
  its pause position, or between the scooter's arrival and the gate.
- **No architectural/construction-logic risk category applies** — this is
  primarily an open road/landscape location with a simple property
  fence/gate, not a complex built structure with load-bearing geometry.
  The equivalent risk here is road/fence-line continuity and hillside
  density, both addressed above.
