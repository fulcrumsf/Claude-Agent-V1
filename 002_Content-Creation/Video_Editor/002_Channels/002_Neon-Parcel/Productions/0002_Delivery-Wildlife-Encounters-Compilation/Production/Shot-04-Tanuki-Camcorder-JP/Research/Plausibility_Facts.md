   mask but canid (not procyonid) skull/body structure, grayish-brown

   roughly cat-to-small-dog sized (~50-60cm body length, ~20-25cm shoulder
   height) — this is a real, stated scale difference from the kangaroo and
   moose calf, not an oversight. Tanuki sightings near rural/suburban

   grounded, not invented.
6. **What is "light rain, wet pavement" actually doing to the scene at

   catching what remaining light there is, light rain visible as fine
   streaks/droplets, the postal scooter's own headlight on given the

7. **What does the road/property itself look like?** A narrow paved rural
