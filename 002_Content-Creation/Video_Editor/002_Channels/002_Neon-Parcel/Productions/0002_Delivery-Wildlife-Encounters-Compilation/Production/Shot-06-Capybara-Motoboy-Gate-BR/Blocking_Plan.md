# Blocking Plan

**Device type: fixed/mounted static CCTV** on the corner of the guardhouse (guarita) at the condo's service entrance. Nobody operates it; nothing pans, tilts or zooms. Only the subjects' paths through the fixed frame are planned.

## Fixed frame (see Data/Site_Plan.md v2 for the full 2D plan)
Camera ~3 m high on a lamp-post bracket at the near curb, looking straight across the delivery bay at the guardhouse, slightly downward, wide-angle (~80 deg) with mild barrel distortion, 2016 HD IP quality, dusk colors with noise, no burned-in text. It sees the courier's FRONT.
- **Foreground:** wet asphalt bay with streetlight reflections and the near curb; the parked motorcycle at lower-LEFT, its LEFT side toward the camera, front wheel toward frame-left, on its sidestand.
- **Center/left, mid-depth:** the guardhouse facade (masonry booth, tinted safety-glass service window with a parcel drawer under it, cool-white light under the concrete awning) and its dry slab (lighter tone) under the projecting awning; the drip line runs across the frame in front of it.
- **Right, mid-depth:** the grass verge with low shrubs along the perimeter wall, joined to the slab's right end by a flush ramp; the capybara is on the grass here from frame 1.
- **Background:** perimeter wall, tropical trees, a sodium streetlight, deep-blue twilight sky.

## Subject origins and routes (nothing appears from nowhere)
- **Motoboy:** already standing under the awning at the service window at frame 1 (arrival not shown), helmet in hand, insulated backpack on his back, motorcycle already parked at the curb (engine off, never moves). His path: stays at the window, then one step sideways and outward past the drip line, ends standing in the drizzle beside the bike side of the lane, helmet on.
- **Capybara:** visible from frame 1, grazing on the grass verge at frame-right (mid-depth). Route: verge -> flush ramp -> right end of the dry slab (continuous, no barrier) -> stops ~1.5-2 m from the courier -> lies down. It never runs, jumps, or touches him, the bike or the backpack.

## Beats (14 s, one continuous uncut shot)
1. 0-3 s: Courier waits under the awning, drizzle falling beyond the drip line; capybara grazes at right.
2. 3-6 s: Capybara lifts its head, walks across the gap toward the slab.
3. 6-8 s: It steps onto the dry slab, stops ~1.5 m from him, sniffs the concrete, lowers forequarters then hindquarters and lies down.
4. 8-11 s: Courier looks down, breathes out, lifts both open hands slightly, steps sideways out from under the awning into the drizzle, keeping it in view.
5. 11-14 s: He puts the helmet on for the rain, stands at the drip line; the capybara's eyes half-close.

## Grounding carried into every prompt (from Research/Plausibility_Facts.md)
Scale: capybara ~1.2 m long / 0.55 m at shoulder (knee-to-thigh height on the courier, shorter than the seat); courier ~1.70 m; bike ~2.0 m; awning clearance ~2.7 m. Capybara is not bothered by rain; it lies on the warm flat slab; the "dry spot" is the courier's belief. Unbranded gear, no readable text or logos anywhere.

## Tone / capture / audio
Unplanned real-life condo security footage, ordinary and observational, never cinematic. Audio: natural location only, drizzle on the awning and pavement, faint distant traffic, soft capybara footfalls and a low breath, the courier's exhale and the helmet strap click, the guardhouse light hum. No dialogue, music or voiceover.

## Hard constraints
Exactly one courier, one capybara, one motorcycle; no other people or animals (porteiro never visible); the motorcycle never moves; the capybara never on/near the bike or backpack; no camera movement; no on-screen text or logos.
