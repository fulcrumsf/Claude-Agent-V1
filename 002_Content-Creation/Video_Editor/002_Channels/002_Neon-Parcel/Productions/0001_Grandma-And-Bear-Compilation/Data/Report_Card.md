## Thumbnail Direction Options

### Option 5 — Swamp Rescue

**Grade: B+**

**Asset:** `Package/Thumbnails/Thumbnail-Option-5-Swamp-Rescue-Text-v1.png`

Tony approved this as a viable thumbnail direction. The successful visual
promise is Grandma's terrified foreground reaction combined with a bear
actively confronting a large alligator behind her. The swamp is softened and
visually subordinate, while Grandma, the bear, and the alligator read as crisp,
separated focal layers. The image is vivid, high-contrast, family-friendly,
and understandable without text.

**Reusable lessons:** Use one instantly readable visual hook; make the primary
emotion large and direct; show the unusual action rather than merely placing
the characters in a scene; separate foreground subjects from a softer
background; and make the thumbnail promise match a real moment represented in
the compilation. This option remains text-free until the overlay is selected.

**Packaging correction:** The overlay and title must describe the full
compilation, not only the swamp-rescue hero moment. Use this image as a vivid
representative example of the recurring Grandma-and-wildlife absurdity. Copy
should be bright, eye-catching, vivid, and poppy while remaining truthful to
the complete clip set.
