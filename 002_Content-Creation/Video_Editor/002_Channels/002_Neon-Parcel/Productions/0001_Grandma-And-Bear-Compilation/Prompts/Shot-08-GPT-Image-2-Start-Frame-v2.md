# Shot 08 Clean Start Frame Prompt

Create one clean 16:9 landscape photorealistic starting frame for an animal-compilation video. This is not a storyboard and must not contain panels, a grid, captions, numbers, borders, labels, or any written text.

Show a fixed elevated outdoor security-camera view of a rural cabin dock on a forested lake in late-afternoon light. The ordinary wooden dock is in the foreground, a single red canoe is loosely tied at the shoreline, and the lake continues toward a bend. No people and no bear are visible yet. Preserve a wide security-camera field of view, mild fisheye distortion, limited dynamic range, subtle grain, imperfect exposure, and non-cinematic realism. Keep the cabin, dock, shoreline, canoe, water, forest, and camera geometry physically believable and clearly composed for later action.
