# Shot 12 Storyboard Prompt

Create a clean 16:9 landscape storyboard for one realistic animal-compilation video clip. Show four chronological panels in a single horizontal row or clearly ordered layout. Put a white caption band beneath each panel with a short description, and keep every number and caption outside the image panels. Do not put any written text, labels, arrows, panel numbers, or storyboard borders inside the visual panels.

Scene: an ordinary suburban backyard at night, viewed from a fixed elevated outdoor security camera. The image should feel like authentic low-light home-security footage: wide field of view, mild fisheye distortion, limited dynamic range, visible grain, imperfect exposure, and no cinematic polish. A fenced yard contains a trampoline with a short set of steps leading up to it.

Panel 1: Three small bear cubs cautiously climb the trampoline steps and step onto the mat. The yard is otherwise empty.
Panel 2: The three cubs make small exploratory hops on the trampoline, each cub visibly separate and realistically scaled.
Panel 3: The cubs become more enthusiastic and bounce together. Headlights briefly wash across the edge of the yard from off-camera, implying that the older woman has arrived home, but do not show a car.
Panel 4: The older woman enters through the fenced gate, climbs the trampoline steps, and joins the cubs with a delighted but believable reaction. End with all four on the trampoline, still distinct and physically grounded.

Visual continuity is the priority: preserve the same elevated security-camera angle, fixed framing, nighttime lighting, fence, trampoline, steps, yard layout, and realistic animal scale across all panels. This is a visual planning reference only; do not make the generated video look like a storyboard or reproduce the panel grid.
