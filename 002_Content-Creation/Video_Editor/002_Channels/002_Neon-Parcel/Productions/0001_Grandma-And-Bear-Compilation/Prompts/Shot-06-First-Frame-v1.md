# Shot 06 First Frame v1

Create one 16:9 photorealistic starting image for a real-life neighbor's smartphone video. This is the exact temporal first frame of the future video, not a storyboard and not a cinematic still.

## TONE AND CAPTURE

Unplanned ordinary home-video footage recorded by a neighbor using a consumer cell phone. Natural late-afternoon daylight, realistic phone exposure, ordinary color, slight imperfect handheld framing, modest phone sharpness, no professional lighting, no cinematic color grade, no commercial polish, no staged advertising look, no tripod, no film-camera depth of field.

## SIMPLE ENVIRONMENT

Show a normal residential front walkway with a simple white picket fence and exactly one narrow pedestrian-sized gate door, wide enough for one person to walk through but not a vehicle. The gate has one continuous rectangular door leaf, one clear hinge side on the left fence post, one latch on the right, and a simple straightforward swing path. No double gate, no second door, no driveway entrance, no car gate, no vehicle, no parked car, no automobile access, and no objects obstructing the gate.

## EXACT FIRST-FRAME STATE

The single white picket gate is fully closed and latched. One large black bear is already clearly visible standing calmly behind the closed gate on the yard side. The bear is grounded on all four paws, fully present from the first frame, and separated from the camera by the fence. Grandma is not in frame yet. No other people, animals, or characters are present.

## CAMERA AND COMPOSITION

One ordinary eye-level phone viewpoint from the public sidewalk outside the fence, aimed directly at the closed pedestrian gate. Keep the entire gate door, both fence posts, latch, hinge side, bear, and surrounding walkway visible. Leave enough room at both side edges for Grandma to enter later, but do not show her now. The image should look like a casual neighbor noticed the bear and pointed a phone at it, not like a professionally composed photograph.

## HARD EXCLUSIONS

No storyboard panels, no captions, no frame numbers, no text, no graphics, no watermark, no cinematic camera angle, no second gate leaf, no double hinges, no car gate, no driveway, no vehicle, no Grandma, no extra people, no extra animals, no duplicate bear, no open gate, no impossible geometry, and no dramatic action.
