# Shot 12 Storyboard Prompt v2

## Overall summary

Create a six-frame storyboard for one continuous, realistic nighttime home-security-camera clip. Three separate bear cubs are already on a backyard trampoline. An older woman enters through the visible backyard gate, walks across the open grass along a clear route, reaches the trampoline steps, climbs the steps, and only then joins the cubs. The storyboard must make every subject origin, route, and physical transition visually explicit so no person can disappear and reappear.

## Global visual contract

- Create one clean 16:9 landscape storyboard sheet with six chronological panels in a clearly ordered layout.
- Every individual image panel must itself be a true 16:9 landscape scene area, not a tall portrait panel.
- Use a wide elevated fixed outdoor security-camera viewpoint that keeps the open gate, the full grass route, the trampoline, and the steps visible together whenever possible.
- Preserve one continuous backyard geometry across all frames: the gate is at the upper-right fence line, the open grass route runs from the gate toward the lower-left trampoline steps, and the trampoline remains in the same position.
- The camera is locked off. Do not change viewpoint, crop, lens, horizon, or spatial layout between frames.
- Show exactly one older woman and exactly three separate bear cubs in every frame where they should be visible. No duplicate people or animals.
- The scene is authentic low-light consumer security-camera footage: mild fisheye distortion, visible grain, imperfect exposure, ordinary backyard lighting, no cinematic polish, no commercial look, no vehicle, and no dramatic camera movement.
- Put a white caption band below each panel. Put the frame number and exact caption only in that band, never inside the image area. Do not put arrows, labels, written text, or graphics inside the visual panels.

## Frame-by-frame sequence

### Frame 1

Wide establishing view of the entire backyard at night. The trampoline and its short steps are clearly visible in the lower-left foreground. Exactly three bear cubs are already on the trampoline mat, visibly separate and cautiously exploring. The backyard gate at the upper-right fence line is closed and empty. Grandma is not yet in the yard.

Caption: Establishing view: three bear cubs are on the trampoline; the closed gate and clear route to the steps are visible.

### Frame 2

The same locked wide view. The three cubs make small exploratory hops on the trampoline. The upper-right gate is now visibly open, but Grandma is still standing just outside the gate and is only partially visible at the threshold. Her body remains outside the yard; she has not teleported into the grass or appeared near the trampoline.

Caption: Grandma opens the gate and is first seen at the gate threshold, before entering the yard.

### Frame 3

The same wide 16:9 view. Exactly one Grandma has fully stepped through the open gate and is now standing on the grass immediately inside it at the upper-right. She faces and looks toward the trampoline. The three cubs remain together on the trampoline at the lower-left. The open grass route between Grandma and the steps is unobstructed and clearly visible.

Caption: Grandma enters through the gate and pauses just inside, looking toward the trampoline and the visible steps.

### Frame 4

The same locked wide view. Grandma is visibly walking across the grass from the upper-right gate toward the lower-left trampoline steps, following the unobstructed route. She is still on the ground and remains several steps away from the trampoline. The gate remains behind her in the same location. The three cubs remain on the trampoline.

Caption: Grandma crosses the open yard along the visible route toward the trampoline steps; she is still on the ground.

### Frame 5

The same locked wide view. Grandma has reached the bottom of the trampoline steps and is standing on the ground directly in front of them, preparing to climb. Her feet are not on the trampoline and she is not yet on the mat. The three cubs remain separate on the trampoline above her.

Caption: Grandma reaches the bottom of the steps and prepares to climb; she has not joined the cubs yet.

### Frame 6

The same locked wide view. Grandma has climbed the short steps and is now safely standing on the trampoline mat beside the three cubs, with all four subjects visibly separate and physically grounded. Her path from the gate through the grass and up the steps is established by the preceding frames. End without another entrance, exit, duplicate Grandma, or extra animal.

Caption: Grandma completes the visible route and joins the three cubs on the trampoline.
