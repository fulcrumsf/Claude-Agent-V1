# Thumbnail Architecture Options

These are three non-destructive, architecture-first options for the approved Neon Parcel long-form compilation. They are planning briefs and generation prompts only; no paid image generation has been run.

## Shared Guardrails

- Canvas: 16:9 YouTube thumbnail, 1280x720 composition.
- Visual promise: realistic-looking, funny home-video moments in which grandmas unexpectedly encounter bears.
- Tone: candid, family-friendly, quirky, warm, mildly chaotic, and documentary rather than polished advertising.
- Use one dominant focal moment, not a six-panel storyboard or a literal collage of every clip.
- Preserve a believable older woman and a believable bear; no duplicate people, merged anatomy, extra limbs, or impossible contact.
- Keep important faces, paws, and objects inside the central mobile-safe area.
- Leave intentional negative space for short text; do not render text in the image-generation step.
- Add final headline typography separately during thumbnail finishing.
- Maintain versions. A rejected or superseded thumbnail is archived, never overwritten.

## Option 1: Direct Faceoff

### Architecture

- Visual promise: Grandma is calmly confronting a bear as if this is a normal household problem.
- Primary focal subject: Grandma on the left, bear on the right, both clearly visible and looking toward each other.
- Foreground: Grandma in a natural three-quarter stance, one hand raised in a practical “go on, get out of here” gesture.
- Midground: Bear at a respectful distance near a simple rural home or yard boundary.
- Background: Softly recognizable residential or rural setting, lightly blurred and uncluttered.
- Scale and position: Medium-large subjects, separated by visible space; bear slightly larger but not oversized.
- Gaze: Grandma looks at the bear; bear looks toward Grandma or the nearby food/object, not at the viewer.
- Lighting and palette: Natural daylight, warm greens and wood tones, realistic phone-camera contrast.
- Reserved text area: Upper-left corner above Grandma, with no face or hand beneath it.
- Exclusions: No multiple grandmas, no extra bears, no action montage, no professional studio lighting, no fake cinematic poster treatment.
- Mobile risk: Subject separation and facial expressions must remain readable when reduced.

### Suggested Headline

`GRANDMA VS. BEAR`

### Image Generation Prompt

```text
Create a 16:9 YouTube thumbnail image for a realistic-looking family home-video compilation about grandmas unexpectedly dealing with bears. One dominant visual moment: a believable older grandmother stands on the left side of a rural home yard, calmly facing one large but natural-looking black bear on the right side near a simple fence or porch boundary. Grandma has a practical mildly annoyed expression and one hand raised as if telling the bear to leave. The bear is clearly separate from her and looks toward her. Natural daylight, candid smartphone-video realism, subtle imperfect framing, warm but not glossy color, lightly blurred background. Keep both subjects fully readable and inside the central mobile-safe area. Leave clean uncluttered negative space in the upper-left above Grandma for a short headline. No text, no subtitles, no logos, no collage, no storyboard panels, no duplicate people, no extra animals, no merged bodies, no deformed limbs, no cinematic advertising look.
```

## Option 2: Unexpected Pattern

### Architecture

- Visual promise: The bear keeps turning up in places where it absolutely should not be.
- Primary focal subject: Bear centered in the foreground, with Grandma reacting in the midground near a house doorway.
- Foreground: Bear facing slightly toward camera, curious and harmless-looking rather than aggressive.
- Midground: Grandma visible from the doorway or yard edge, pointing toward the bear with a surprised but comedic reaction.
- Background: Simple home exterior with one or two recognizable cues, such as a porch, gate, trampoline, or garden hose; never all at once.
- Scale and position: Bear large enough to identify; Grandma smaller but unmistakable and not hidden.
- Gaze: Grandma looks directly at the bear, not at an absent off-camera subject.
- Lighting and palette: Real outdoor daylight with natural shadows and modest phone-camera exposure.
- Reserved text area: Left-center open wall/sky area, away from the bear’s face.
- Exclusions: No visual implication that Grandma and the bear are in the same impossible physical position; no cluttered prop pile; no duplicate Grandma.
- Mobile risk: Bear silhouette must not merge with the house or dark background.

### Suggested Headline

`HE KEEPS SHOWING UP`

### Image Generation Prompt

```text
Create a 16:9 YouTube thumbnail image for a realistic-looking, family-friendly home-video compilation. Show one curious black bear clearly in the foreground of a simple residential yard, centered slightly right, with believable anatomy and paws. In the midground, one older grandmother has just stepped out of a visible house doorway and is looking directly at the bear with a surprised, comedic expression while pointing toward it. The doorway establishes exactly where she came from. Use only one simple environmental cue, such as a small porch and garden edge, so the scene remains physically clear. Natural candid smartphone-camera realism, ordinary daylight, subtle handheld-documentary feel, warm natural color, not polished or commercial. Leave clean negative space on the left-center for a short headline. No text, no subtitles, no logos, no storyboard panels, no montage, no duplicate grandmother, no extra people, no extra bears, no impossible positions, no deformed limbs, no glossy advertisement lighting.
```

## Option 3: Caught-On-Camera Reaction

### Architecture

- Visual promise: These are unbelievable-looking but funny moments captured by ordinary people.
- Primary focal subject: Grandma in the foreground reacting toward a bear just behind her or across the yard.
- Foreground: Grandma turned three-quarter toward the bear, with a clear expressive reaction and grounded stance.
- Midground: One bear in a separate, believable location, such as near a gate, porch, or sprinkler.
- Background: A simple real home-video setting with mild visual imperfections; avoid a dramatic action scene.
- Scale and position: Grandma and bear balanced diagonally so both read at thumbnail size.
- Gaze: Grandma’s eyes and gesture point toward the bear; bear remains visually distinct.
- Lighting and palette: Slightly imperfect natural exposure, neutral daylight, restrained saturation.
- Reserved text area: Upper-right sky/wall area, away from the bear.
- Exclusions: No security-camera overlay, timestamp, or fake interface unless added deliberately in post; no split screen or six-panel layout.
- Mobile risk: Do not let the candid framing become too wide for either subject to read.

### Suggested Headline

`CAUGHT ON CAMERA`

### Image Generation Prompt

```text
Create a 16:9 YouTube thumbnail image for a realistic-looking family home-video compilation featuring funny encounters between grandmas and bears. One older grandmother is in the foreground on the left, standing naturally and turned three-quarter toward one clearly visible black bear in the midground on the right near a simple residential gate or porch. Grandma has a highly readable surprised-but-comedic reaction and is looking directly at the bear. The bear is physically separate, grounded on the same outdoor surface, and looking back toward her. Compose it like an ordinary person captured the moment on a phone: subtle imperfect framing, natural daylight, realistic exposure, modest contrast, no professional commercial polish. Leave clean negative space in the upper-right for a short headline. No text, no subtitles, no logos, no split screen, no storyboard panels, no duplicate people, no extra animals, no impossible anatomy, no floating objects, no cinematic trailer look, no staged advertising composition.
```

## Selection Guidance

- Best first test: Option 1, because the faceoff communicates the compilation premise fastest and uses the fewest environmental assumptions.
- Best curiosity hook: Option 2, because the doorway origin and recurring-bear idea create an immediate question.
- Best home-video positioning: Option 3, because the candid composition reinforces the channel’s “real people, strange moments” identity.

## Validation Before Generation

- Confirm the headline is truthful to the final compilation and not promising a single event that does not appear.
- Inspect the generated image for one Grandma, one bear, believable separation, natural gaze direction, intact limbs, and a clear path/ground plane.
- Check the thumbnail at mobile size and against both light and dark YouTube backgrounds.
- Run one meaningful revision at a time; archive each superseded version.
