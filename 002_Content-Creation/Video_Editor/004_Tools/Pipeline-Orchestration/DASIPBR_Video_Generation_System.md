# DASIPBR Video Generation System

## Purpose

This document defines the orchestration and specification-extraction framework for building autonomous AI-powered viral video generation pipelines.

This system is NOT the final pipeline itself.

This system is:
- the orchestration layer
- the planning layer
- the specification extraction layer
- the viral structure analysis layer
- the storytelling intelligence layer

The purpose of this framework is to:
1. Interview the creator
2. Extract rough ideas conversationally
3. Convert rough ideas into structured story premises
4. Analyze viral reference material
5. Understand visual storytelling mechanics
6. Understand retention engineering
7. Build execution plans
8. Generate production specifications
9. Coordinate specialized agents/tools
10. Eventually orchestrate end-to-end video generation and publishing

---

# Core Concept

The creator should be able to roughly dictate an idea like:

> "A grandma finds a dirty abandoned bear cub near her mailbox during winter. She rescues it, raises it, and years later the bear returns with its family."

The system should:
- understand the premise
- clean the premise
- understand emotional intent
- understand platform style
- understand pacing
- understand visual logic
- understand POV consistency
- understand transformation arcs
- understand retention mechanics
- understand thumbnail psychology
- generate production-ready outputs

The system should NOT interpret rough dictation literally.

It should:
- infer intent
- preserve emotional meaning
- improve structure
- strengthen retention
- maintain realism consistency

# DASIPBR Framework

## DASIPBR
### Declare → Assess → Isolate → Process → Build → Reveal

DASIPBR is a:
- retention-engineering framework
- pacing framework
- narrative sequencing framework
- emotional escalation framework
- completion-compulsion framework

It is NOT limited to one niche.

It can apply to:
- emotional rescue stories
- absurd realism
- process videos
- transformation videos
- documentary styles
- cinematic shorts
- comedy
- horror
- AI-generated realism

---

# DASIPBR Breakdown

## 1. DECLARE

### Purpose
Immediately establish:
- the premise
- the emotional hook
- the curiosity loop
- the transformation promise

### Psychological Goal
The viewer should instantly understand:
- what is happening
- what might happen
- why they should keep watching

### Important Rule
The DECLARE phase should visually function as:
- the opening hook
- the thumbnail
- the first-second frame
- the curiosity engine

For YouTube Shorts:
The first frame IS effectively the thumbnail.

### Example
- Grandma discovers dirty abandoned bear cub near mailbox
- Security camera footage captures discovery

### Key Principles
- instantly understandable
- emotionally charged
- visually readable
- curiosity-inducing
- transformation-implying

---

## 2. ASSESS

### Purpose
Analyze and emotionally establish the problem.

### Psychological Goal
Increase emotional investment.

### Example
- Bear is cold
- dirty
- hungry
- weak
- alone

### Visual Techniques
- closeups
- POV phone footage
- documentary realism
- observational framing

---

## 3. ISOLATE

### Purpose
Focus tightly on solving specific problems.

### Psychological Goal
Create process curiosity.

### Example
- bathing the cub
- removing dirt
- drying fur
- feeding carefully
- cleaning wounds

### Key Principle
The viewer begins emotionally attaching to the transformation.

---

## 4. PROCESS

### Purpose
Show progression and satisfying transformation mechanics.

### Psychological Goal
Create progression dopamine.

### Example
- healing montage
- playful moments
- feeding progression
- learning behaviors
- environmental adaptation

### Important
This section often contains:
- peaks and valleys
- pacing rhythm
- escalating emotional connection

---

## 5. BUILD

### Purpose
Escalate toward emotional payoff.

### Psychological Goal
Increase anticipation.

### Example
- bear becomes healthy
- release preparation
- emotional separation
- return to wilderness

### Important
The audience should begin anticipating:
- the final emotional reward
- the reveal
- completion

---

## 6. REVEAL

### Purpose
Deliver emotional payoff.

### Psychological Goal
Release emotional tension.

### Example
- years later
- snowy morning
- bear returns with family
- emotional reunion

### Important
The reveal should feel:
- emotionally earned
- visually rewarding
- transformational
- memorable

# Visual Logic Rules

Every scene should answer:
- Who is filming?
- Why are they filming?
- Is the camera believable?
- Is the POV emotionally consistent?
- Does the footage feel real?
- Does the scene escalate emotion?

Example:
A grandma going to the mailbox would not already be filming with a phone.

Therefore:
- security camera footage makes sense first
- cellphone footage begins after discovery

The system must preserve this realism logic.
