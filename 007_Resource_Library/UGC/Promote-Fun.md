## Summary
Promote.fun is a performance-based content distribution marketplace that connects brands with creators for short-form video campaigns across TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube. It enables creators to monetize their content distribution by earning based on verified views, while brands achieve authentic reach and campaign performance.
