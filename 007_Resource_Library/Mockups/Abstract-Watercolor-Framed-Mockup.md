## Summary
A staged room mockup featuring a large, vertically oriented abstract art print in a light wood frame. The artwork displays fluid, overlapping shapes with a watercolor texture in shades of blue, teal, pink, orange, and yellow against a cream background. The setting includes white classical wainscoting and herringbone parquet flooring, designed as a blank canvas for superimposing custom designs.
