# Video Prompt 1

"This video prompt was used with three reference images: character sheets as @image2 and @image3, and an environment sheet as @image1."

```
[CINEMATIC SETUP]

ARRI Alexa 65, anamorphic 50mm T2.0, shallow DOF, handheld with slow dolly. Grade: warm amber highlights, indigo shadows, crushed blacks, filmic grain. Lighting: warm tungsten through shoji as key, firelight from hearth as secondary. Atmosphere: cherry petals drifting, incense curl. Audio: footfalls, shoji slide, robe rustle, fire crackle, fabric slip, soft breath — sound FX only, no music, no dialogue. Face stable, no deformation.

[@image1] is the environment — washitsu on a straight front-to-back axis: sliding door at front, red zabuton, sunken hearth with kettle and embers, blue zabuton, tokonoma with plum blossoms at back. [@image2] is the son — samurai 20s, gray-charcoal robes, white crest, hair tied back. [@image3] is the father — samurai 50s, black robes, family crest, graying hair tied back, gray beard, black blindfold in every shot. Blindfold stays tied throughout — never removed, never touched, never slips. He never cries, cheeks dry throughout. Father sits on red zabuton facing the door with hearth directly behind him. Son sits on blue zabuton behind the hearth, facing the father across the fire. Positions never swap.

0-2s: Wide establishing from the front at eye level, looking down the axis — empty red zabuton in foreground, hearth glowing behind it, empty blue zabuton further back, tokonoma at the end. Petals drifting. Slow push-in along the axis. Fire crackle, petal landing.

2-5s: Medium on [@image3] on the red zabuton facing camera, hearth glowing directly behind him with kettle steam rising, painting his silhouette in warm rim light. Sheathed katana across his knees, blindfold firmly tied. Quiet natural sadness — mouth downturned, jaw heavy, cheeks dry. Slow breath lifts his shoulders. Ember pop, measured exhale.

5-7s: Reverse angle behind [@image3] looking past the hearth toward the sliding door. The door glides open; [@image2] steps through into warm hallway glow. He bows deeply. Father's shoulders, glowing hearth, and son's arrival on one line. Wood slide, soft footfall.

7-9s: Handheld drift alongside [@image2] at shoulder height as he walks past [@image3] and the hearth, heading toward the blue zabuton. His face passes through frame — a tear breaks free down his cheek, jaw working. Footsteps, cloth, fire crackle.

9-11s: Side profile two-shot from the left wall at tatami level, full axis in frame — [@image2] lowering onto the blue zabuton at back, hearth in the middle, [@image3] on the red zabuton closest to camera, firelight between the two men. Son bows his head, shoulders trembling once before stilling. Father's blindfolded head tilting as if listening. Sadness unchanged. Petal landing, shared exhale.

11-13s: Slow tracking arc from side profile around to three-quarter front on [@image3], hearth glowing behind his shoulder. He lifts the sheathed katana and extends it forward over the fire toward his son. Expression softens — mouth easing, jaw loosening. Blindfold in place, cheeks dry. Firelight catches the scabbard from below. Steady exhale, wood creak.

13-15s: Macro on tsuba and silk cord as [@image3]'s hands lower the blade over the hearth into [@image2]'s trembling palms on the far side, embers glowing out of focus beneath. Focus pulls through the blade to [@image2]'s head — it breaks, tears spilling, brow creasing, soundless breath. A petal onto the scabbard. Slow crane up revealing the full axis from above — father on red with hearth behind, katana now in son's hands on blue, tokonoma at back. Father's palms open on his knees, blindfold intact, face calm and neutral, at peace. [@image2] bows his head, tears on the scabbard. Held beat. Cut to black.
```
