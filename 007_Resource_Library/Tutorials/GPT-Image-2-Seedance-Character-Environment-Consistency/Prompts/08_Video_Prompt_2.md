# Video Prompt 2

"This video prompt was used with three reference images: character sheets as @image2 and @image3, and an environment sheet as @image1."

```
[CINEMATIC SETUP]

ARRI Alexa 65, anamorphic 50mm T2.0, shallow DOF, handheld with slow dolly. Grade: warm amber highlights, indigo shadows, crushed blacks, filmic grain. Lighting: warm tungsten through shoji as key, firelight from hearth as secondary. Atmosphere: cherry petals drifting, incense curl. Audio: footfalls, shoji slide, robe rustle, fire crackle, fabric slip, soft breath — sound FX only, no music, no dialogue. Face stable, no deformation.

[@image1] is the environment — washitsu, tatami, shoji walls, tokonoma with plum blossoms, central hearth with embers, red zabuton on one side and blue zabuton on the other. [@image2] is the son — samurai 20s, gray-charcoal robes, white crest, hair tied back. [@image3] is the father — samurai 50s, black robes, family crest, graying hair tied back, gray beard, black blindfold in every shot. Blindfold stays tied throughout — never removed, never touched, never slips. He never cries, cheeks dry throughout. Father sits on red zabuton; son sits on blue zabuton. Colors never swap.

0-2s: Extreme close-up on hearth embers pulsing, heat shimmer rising. Slow rack focus pulls to reveal [@image3] on the red zabuton in the background, blindfolded, katana across his knees. Fire crackle, petal landing.

2-5s: Dutch-tilted medium on [@image3] through drifting foreground petals. Quiet sadness — mouth downturned, jaw heavy, cheeks dry. Firelight flickers across his weathered face. Slow breath lifts his shoulders. Ember pop, measured exhale.

5-7s: Reflection shot on the polished iron kettle above the embers. Sliding door glides open in the curved reflection, distorted [@image2] stepping through. Focus pulls to the real door behind. [@image2] bows, steps onto tatami. Wood slide, soft footfall.

7-9s: Handheld drift alongside [@image2] at shoulder height as he walks toward the hearth. His face passes through frame — a tear breaks free down his cheek, jaw working. Footsteps, cloth, fire crackle.

9-11s: Low profile two-shot at tatami level, hearth between them. [@image2] lowers onto the blue zabuton facing his father, head bowed, shoulders trembling once before stilling. [@image3] on the red zabuton opposite, blindfolded head tilting as if listening, sadness unchanged. Petal landing, shared exhale.

11-13s: Slow tracking arc around the hearth to three-quarter front on [@image3]. He lifts the sheathed katana and presents it at chest height. Expression softens — mouth easing, jaw loosening. Blindfold in place, cheeks dry. Firelight catches the scabbard. Steady exhale, wood creak.

13-15s: Macro on tsuba and silk cord as [@image3]'s hands lower the blade slowly into [@image2]'s trembling palms, embers glowing out of focus below. Focus pulls through the blade to [@image2]'s eyes — it breaks, tears spilling, brow creasing, soundless breath. A petal onto the scabbard. Slow crane up revealing both kneeling on either side of the hearth — [@image3] on red, [@image2] on blue, katana in the son's hands, father's palms open on his knees, blindfold intact, face calm and neutral, at peace. [@image2] bows his head, tears on the scabbard. Held beat. Cut to black.
```
