Cinematic wide interior establishing shot of a traditional Japanese ancestral family room (washitsu) at golden hour. Empty, deserted, reverent stillness. Intimate human scale. Shot in 16:9 widescreen anamorphic.

OBJECTS (clear, unduplicated, correctly scaled)

Wide establishing composition at roughly eye-level kneeling height, framed so both cushions are visible in symmetrical balance with the tokonoma centered in the background.
