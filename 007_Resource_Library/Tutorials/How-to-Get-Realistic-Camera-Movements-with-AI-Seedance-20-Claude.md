AI video generations often struggle with organic camera motion—they can quickly feel floaty, static, or unnatural. In this episode, I’m putting Seedance 2.0 to the test to see how far we can push its motion controls for an intense action chase sequence!  
  
Using Claude as a virtual director to craft motion-heavy prompts, we force realistic handheld movement directly inside Seedance 2.0. Stick around to the end for a quick post-production tip on how stacking camera shake plugins can give your final clip that extra layer of visceral weight and impact.

CAMERA DETAIL EXAMPLE: ( May work with other video models but not tested yet)  
  
Camera: on the operator's shoulder, sprinting backward at full speed  
and barely holding the frame. Long lens compressing the depth of the  
lane. Heavy vertical jolts on every footfall. Sharp lateral whip  
corrections that overshoot and snap back. The runner slides off-centre  
and is wrestled back into frame. Rolling dutch tilts from the  
operator's own stride. Momentary framing losses where his head clips  
the top of frame. Aggressive focus hunting as he closes on the lens.  
Bodies cut across the foreground and briefly wipe frame. Raw,  
unstabilised, reactive — a frame grabbed while running, not composed.  
  
Exposed for the highlights, shadows crushed to black. Bold saturated  
colour, hard practical sources doing the work. Clean and sharp edge to  
edge. No CGI gloss, no tidy symmetry.
