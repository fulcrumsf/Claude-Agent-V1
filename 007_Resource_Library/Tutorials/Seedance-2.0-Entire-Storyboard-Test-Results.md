In this video I tested a completely different AI video workflow —  
instead of generating scenes one by one, I built a full storyboard  
first and fed the entire thing to the AI with a single prompt.  
  
I tested this across 5 different projects  
For each project I used 2 AI tools to generate the storyboard:  
GPT Image 2  
Nano Banana 2  
  
Then fed the storyboard into Seedance 2.0 to generate the final video.  
  
Let's be honest about when this works and when it doesn't
