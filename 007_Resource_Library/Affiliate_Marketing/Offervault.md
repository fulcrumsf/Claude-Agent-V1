## Summary
OfferVault is a free online search engine and aggregator for affiliate and CPA (Cost Per Action) offers from various affiliate networks. It allows affiliate marketers to find, compare, and research thousands of offers based on criteria like payout, category, and network, helping them identify suitable campaigns to promote. It is not an affiliate network itself, but a reference tool to discover offers and connect with the respective networks.
