## Summary
Zeydoo is a global CPA (Cost Per Action) affiliate network that provides a platform for publishers and affiliates to monetize their traffic. It offers various in-house products, including SmartLink, surveys, sweepstakes, and mobile applications, designed to optimize conversion rates across diverse traffic sources and geographical regions. The platform facilitates campaign management, tracking, and bi-weekly payouts for its partners.
