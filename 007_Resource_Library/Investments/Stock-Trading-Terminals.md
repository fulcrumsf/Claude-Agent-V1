## Summary
Trading Terminal is a trading journal and analytics platform built for forex and futures traders, with a strong focus on prop firm challenges and funded accounts. It provides tools for market analysis, real-time trading simulation, market replay, advanced trade scanning, and live audio breaking news. The platform also includes features like a full trade journal with over 30 fields per trade, a performance dashboard, and deep analytics.
