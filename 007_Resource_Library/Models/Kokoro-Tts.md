## Summary
Kokoro TTS is an advanced AI text-to-speech model with 82 million parameters, built on StyleTTS 2 architecture, that delivers high-quality, natural-sounding speech synthesis. It is designed for efficient and fast audio generation, supporting multiple languages and running efficiently on CPUs.
