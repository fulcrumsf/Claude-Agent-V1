## Summary
A black-and-white line drawing of a human figure in profile, utilizing a continuous single-line technique for the torso and shoulder while employing chaotic, overlapping scribbles to render the hair and facial features. The image serves as a visual reference for an abstract portraiture style where organic lines define form without traditional shading or solid fills.
