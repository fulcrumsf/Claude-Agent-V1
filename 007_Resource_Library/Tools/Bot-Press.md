## Summary
Botpress is an AI agent and chatbot platform for building, deploying, and managing LLM-powered conversational experiences across various channels. It provides a cloud-based Agent Studio for designing conversation flows, orchestration logic, and integrations. The platform is used for automating customer support, e-commerce assistance, internal helpdesks, and workflows.
