## Summary
Arcads AI is a browser-based SaaS platform that generates video advertisements featuring AI actors. It allows users to create UGC-style video ads by inputting scripts and selecting from a library of AI actors, streamlining the ad creative production process. The platform also offers features like voice tools, localization, and product visuals.
