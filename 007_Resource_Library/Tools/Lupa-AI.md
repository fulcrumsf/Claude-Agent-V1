## Summary
Lupa AI Upscaler is a web-based service that utilizes advanced artificial intelligence to enhance and enlarge digital images. It improves image resolution, sharpens details, and intelligently reconstructs missing information for clearer, higher-quality visuals. The tool is designed for creative professionals needing to transform low-resolution assets into high-quality images suitable for various uses.
