## Summary
Convergence.ai developed Proxy, an AI-powered digital assistant designed to automate complex web-based tasks by interacting with websites like a human. It leverages Large Meta Learning Models for continuous learning, long-term memory, and adaptability to dynamic interfaces. Salesforce acquired Convergence.ai to enhance its AI capabilities and advance its Agentforce platform.
