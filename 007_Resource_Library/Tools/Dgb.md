## Summary
DGB.lol is a free online platform offering a suite of AI-powered image manipulation tools, including an AI image upscaler, background remover, and RGB to CMYK converter. It is designed for various uses, such as preparing images for print-on-demand, enhancing resolution, and processing Midjourney outputs. The platform prioritizes user privacy by automatically deleting processed images after 24 hours.
