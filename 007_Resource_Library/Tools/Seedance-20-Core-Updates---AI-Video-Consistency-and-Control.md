Hi there,

If you’ve ever generated a “great” clip—then watched it fall apart when you needed *one more shot* —you already know the hard parts of AI video:

- the face drifts between shots
- actions don’t match intent
- extensions feel like patchwork
- editing one section forces a full remake
- beat sync is never quite on-beat

**Seedance 2.0’s Core Updates are designed to solve these “hard problems” with multimodal referencing (image / video / audio / text): you show what to follow, then describe what to change.**

Below are **7 real cases** to give you the exact mental model.
