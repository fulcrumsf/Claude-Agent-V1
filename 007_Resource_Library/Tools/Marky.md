## Summary
MyMarky AI is an AI-powered social media marketing platform that automates content creation and scheduling across multiple platforms. It generates on-brand text and image content, including carousels, and allows users to schedule posts in minutes. The tool aims to save time for businesses and individuals by streamlining their social media presence.
