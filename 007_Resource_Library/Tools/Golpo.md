## Summary
Golpo AI is an AI-powered video creation platform that converts documents, text, or prompts into professional whiteboard animation videos. It offers features like various video styles, narration options, and frame-by-frame editing, catering to needs in education, marketing, and training. The platform aims to simplify video production without requiring technical skills or extensive editing experience.
