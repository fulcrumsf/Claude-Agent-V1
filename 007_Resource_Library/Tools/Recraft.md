## Summary
Recraft AI is a web-based generative artificial intelligence design platform that creates and edits visual assets, including raster images and scalable vector graphics (SVG), from text prompts. It emphasizes brand consistency through custom style controls and offers features for generating logos, illustrations, icons, and mockups.
