## Summary
Hedra is an AI content studio that generates character-led videos, images, and audio from text and images, focusing on brand consistency. It utilizes an AI agent to manage creative workflows, from ideation to content generation and editing. The platform provides a browser-based workspace and a developer API, leveraging proprietary models like Character-3 for expressive human characters.
