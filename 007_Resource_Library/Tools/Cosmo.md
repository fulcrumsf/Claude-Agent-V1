## Summary
Cosmos.so is a digital platform designed for creatives to discover, save, organize, and share inspiration, functioning as a distraction-free alternative to Pinterest. Users can save various content types like images, links, videos, and articles into organized 'clusters' and collaborate on collections. It is accessible via web, mobile apps, and browser extensions, featuring AI-powered tagging and Pinterest import capabilities.
