## Summary
ImagePrompt.org is an AI-powered platform designed to assist users in creating and optimizing text prompts for AI image generation tools such as Midjourney, Stable Diffusion, and Flux. It allows users to convert ideas or uploaded images into detailed prompts, simplifying the process of generating AI art. The platform also includes an AI Image Generator.
