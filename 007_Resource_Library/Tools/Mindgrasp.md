## Summary
Mindgrasp AI is an online AI learning assistant that transforms uploaded educational materials, including lectures, documents, and videos, into structured study aids. It generates notes, summaries, flashcards, and quizzes, and offers an AI tutor to answer questions based on the uploaded content. The platform aims to enhance learning efficiency and assist with exam preparation.
