## Summary
RenderNet AI is an AI-powered platform that generates consistent, high-quality characters and product ads for social media platforms. It offers features like multilingual lip-sync, pose replication, and various style transformations for images and videos. The platform now redirects to Affogato, an all-in-one AI studio focused on maintaining character and product consistency across diverse content types for creators and marketers.
