## Summary
Voluum is a cloud-based performance marketing platform designed for advertisers, affiliate marketers, and media buying teams. It provides an all-in-one solution to track, analyze, optimize, and automate online advertising campaigns across various channels. The platform helps users manage traffic, conversions, and A/B tests to maximize ROI and prevent fraud.
