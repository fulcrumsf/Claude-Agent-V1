## Summary
Rosebud AI is a browser-based game creation platform that enables users to prototype and publish 2D and 3D games using natural language prompts, often referred to as 'vibe coding'. It generates game code, visual, and audio assets, allowing creators to turn ideas into playable experiences quickly without traditional coding. The platform supports rapid iteration and is suitable for indie developers, educators, and artists for creating games, simulations, and interactive apps.
