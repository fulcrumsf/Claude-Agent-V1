## Summary
AudioPen is an AI-powered voice-to-text tool that converts spoken content into polished, structured written text. It goes beyond basic transcription by rewriting and formatting notes in various styles and languages, making it suitable for note-taking, content creation, and general writing tasks. It is available as a web app, and also has iOS and Android apps, and a Chrome extension.
