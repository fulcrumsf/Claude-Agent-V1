## Summary
ProductionCrate is a subscription-based online library providing visual effects (VFX) assets, motion graphics, 3D models, royalty-free music, and sound effects for video editing and post-production. It also offers plugins and a desktop application to streamline workflows for filmmakers, content creators, and VFX artists.
