## Summary
Genspark is an all-in-one AI workspace and 'Super Agent' that leverages multiple large language models and over 80 pre-built tools to automate a wide range of tasks. It can generate various content types like documents, presentations, images, and code, and perform actions such as making phone calls and analyzing data.
