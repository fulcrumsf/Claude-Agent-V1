## Summary
Feedough's AI Product Requirements Document (PRD) Generator is an online AI tool designed to help product managers and teams quickly create structured PRDs. It uses generative AI to transform basic product details into a comprehensive document outlining the product's scope, features, goals, and requirements. The tool streamlines the PRD creation process by breaking it down into input, process, and output steps.
