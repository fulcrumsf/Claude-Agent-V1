## Summary
OpusClip is an AI-powered video repurposing tool that transforms long videos into short, viral-ready clips for social media platforms like TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts. It automatically identifies highlights, generates captions, reframes videos for vertical formats, and allows direct publishing or scheduling.
