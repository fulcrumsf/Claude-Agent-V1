## Summary
MMaudio is an AI-powered platform that generates various audio content, including natural AI voices and sound effects. It offers text-to-speech with emotion control and zero-shot voice cloning, and can also synthesize synchronized sound effects and background audio for videos by analyzing visual content. Users can also create custom audio from text prompts.
