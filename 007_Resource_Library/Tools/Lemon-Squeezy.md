## Summary
Lemon Squeezy is an all-in-one e-commerce platform designed for selling digital products, subscriptions, and software licenses. It functions as a Merchant of Record, handling payments, global tax compliance, and fraud prevention for its users. The platform also includes tools for marketing, subscription management, and analytics to support online businesses.
