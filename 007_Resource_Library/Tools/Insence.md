## Summary
Insense is a user-generated content (UGC) and influencer marketing platform that connects brands with creators. It streamlines the process of sourcing creators, managing campaigns, handling payments, and securing content rights for paid social media ads on platforms like TikTok and Meta. The platform is designed to help brands scale their UGC production and influencer marketing activities.
