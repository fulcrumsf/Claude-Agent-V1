## Summary
Relevance AI is a low-code and no-code platform for creating, deploying, and managing AI agents and multi-agent teams. It enables businesses to automate complex workflows across various functions like sales, marketing, and operations. The platform allows users to build specialized AI agents, equip them with tools and knowledge, and connect them into 'Workforces' to autonomously complete tasks.
