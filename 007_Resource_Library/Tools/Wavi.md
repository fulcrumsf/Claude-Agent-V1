## Summary
Wava AI is an online AI-powered video generator designed to create short-form, faceless content for social media platforms like TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and Instagram Reels. Users input a script or prompt, and the tool automatically generates a video with AI narration, background stock clips, and synchronized captions, aiming for viral potential without requiring traditional editing skills.
