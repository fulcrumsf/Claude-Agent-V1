## Summary
Awesome Nano Banana is a GitHub repository that serves as a curated collection of images and prompts generated by Google's Gemini 2.5 Flash image generation and editing model, also known as Nano Banana. It showcases the advanced capabilities of Gemini in AI image generation and editing, providing examples and inspiration for users.
