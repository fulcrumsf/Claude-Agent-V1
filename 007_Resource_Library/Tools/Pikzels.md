## Summary
Pikzels is an AI-powered platform designed to help YouTube creators generate, test, and iterate on video thumbnails and titles. It aims to improve click-through rates by offering features like AI thumbnail generation, title optimization, face swapping, and performance scoring. The tool focuses on YouTube-specific performance rather than general image generation.
