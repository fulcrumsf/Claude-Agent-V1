## Summary
Sanity.io is an open-source, cloud-based headless CMS that enables users to manage, edit, and deliver structured content across various devices and platforms. It offers a flexible data model, a customizable editing interface called Sanity Studio, and APIs for efficient content delivery to modern frontends.
