## Summary
ShineOn is a print-on-demand (POD) platform specializing in personalized jewelry and gifts. It enables entrepreneurs to design, sell, and fulfill custom jewelry products through their own e-commerce stores, handling production, packaging, and shipping. The platform integrates with major e-commerce platforms like Shopify and Etsy, offering tools for product creation, marketing, and order management.
