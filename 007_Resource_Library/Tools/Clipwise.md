## Summary
Clipwise.ai is an AI-powered video generator that creates short-form videos from text prompts. It automates script creation, media selection, voiceover generation in multiple languages, and subtitle integration. The tool is designed for content creators and businesses to produce high-quality videos efficiently for platforms like YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, and TikTok.
