## Summary
Social Army is a paid community and educational platform hosted on Whop, primarily designed for TikTok Shop affiliates. It provides courses, tutorials, resources, and community support to help members scale their social media presence and monetize content on TikTok Shop. The platform focuses on strategies for content conversion, scaling, and building a loyal audience for affiliate marketing.
