# beehiiv MCP

> Email from **Tyler from beehiiv** about the launch of the **beehiiv MCP**, signup flows, and the website dashboard.

## Quick Summary

beehiiv announced a new **beehiiv MCP** integration that connects a beehiiv account to AI clients such as Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, and other tools that support the **Model Context Protocol**.

The main idea is that instead of copying and pasting newsletter data into an AI chat, the AI can connect directly to beehiiv and analyze account data. Version 1 is read-only. A future version is planned to include write access.

The email also announces updates to **Signup Flows** and the **Website Dashboard**.

---

## What the beehiiv MCP Does

Until now, if you wanted AI to analyze your newsletter, it had to piece together an answer from whatever data you pasted in or shared. It was only as useful as the snapshot you gave it.

The **beehiiv MCP** changes that.

MCP is an open standard that lets AI tools connect directly to software like beehiiv. No more copying and pasting into a chat window. Now, your AI can plug into your account and access, analyze, and act on everything inside it.

This means deeper insights and more tactical support for your business. For example, you can ask ChatGPT to break down who’s reading your newsletter: the types of companies, their size, and how that’s changing over time. From there, it can use that data to help build a media kit for potential sponsors.

That’s beyond what a traditional dashboard can do because it requires context, reasoning, and the ability to connect dots across the entire dataset.

## Why Now?

As tools like Claude, ChatGPT, and Gemini become places where people get work done, AI interfaces are becoming a new kind of dashboard.

beehiiv says the MCP is how a content business can show up inside those AI tools.

## beehiiv MCP v1

Version 1 starts with **read-only access**.

Example questions mentioned in the email:

- Who are my most engaged free subscribers who haven’t upgraded, and what do they have in common?
- Audit my website and give me a prioritized list of SEO fixes based on my content and structure.
- Are there any unusual churn or unsubscribe spikes in the last 90 days? What posts or dates correlate with them?

## Example Workflows

The email gives examples of using beehiiv insights across other tools:

- Every Monday at 8am, pull last week’s revenue across ads, subscriptions, and digital products, then post a week-over-week summary to **Slack**.
- When a subscriber hits 10+ opens and 5+ clicks, create or update their contact in **HubSpot** and enroll them in an active sales sequence.
- Every Friday, pull this week’s send performance, summarize what worked, draft a Monday editorial brief in **Gmail**, and create a 9am meeting invite via **Google Calendar**.

# Signup Flows

Signup flows give creators control over the early subscriber experience. beehiiv says this is the first opportunity to make a new reader feel like they made a good decision.

The update allows users to create **multi-step signup flows** that guide subscribers through a customized onboarding experience.

Example default signup flow from the email:

1. **Recommendation modal** — drives traffic to favorite newsletters.
2. **Onboarding survey** — collects data about readers.
3. **Welcome page** — welcomes new subscribers and sets expectations.

beehiiv says different signup flows can be created for different subscriber journeys. For example, someone who joins through a lead magnet can receive a different onboarding flow than someone who subscribes directly from the site.

**Create signup flows:**  
https://app.beehiiv.com/website_builder_v2/settings/signup_flows

**Note from email:** The new Signup Flow builder is only available to users on the v2 Website Builder and is not compatible with the legacy builder.

# Website Dashboard
