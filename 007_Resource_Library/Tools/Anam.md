## Summary
Anam.ai is an AI platform that enables the creation and deployment of real-time, interactive, and photorealistic AI avatars (personas). It provides APIs and SDKs for embedding these emotionally expressive, multilingual digital humans into various applications. The platform focuses on delivering human-like communication experiences with ultra-low latency for use cases like customer support, sales, and education.
