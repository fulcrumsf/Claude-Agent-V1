## Summary
Subliminator is a print-on-demand (POD) service specializing in all-over print (AOP) apparel and accessories. It provides tools for users to design custom products, generate 3D mockups, and integrate with e-commerce platforms like Shopify and Etsy. The service handles production and fulfillment of custom-printed orders, allowing businesses to sell products without managing inventory.
