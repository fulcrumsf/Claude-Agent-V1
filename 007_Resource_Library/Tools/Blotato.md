## Summary
Blotato is an AI-powered social media content repurposing and publishing platform. It transforms long-form content into platform-specific short-form posts and generates AI faceless videos with AI voices and images for various social media channels. The tool also offers content scheduling and an API for integration with other AI agents for automated workflows.
