## Summary
Virlo is a short-form video analytics platform that tracks trends, creators, and competitors across TikTok, YouTube Shorts, and Instagram Reels. It provides insights to guide content creation and repurposing strategies for short-form video content. Virlo also offers an API for developers to integrate its intelligence into their own applications.
