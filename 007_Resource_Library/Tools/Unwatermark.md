## Summary
Unwatermark.ai is an AI-powered online tool designed to remove watermarks, logos, text, and other unwanted objects from images and videos. It utilizes advanced algorithms to automatically detect and erase these elements while aiming to preserve the original quality of the media. The tool supports various image and video formats and offers both free and premium features.
