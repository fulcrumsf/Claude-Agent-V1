## Summary
Immersity AI is a platform by Leia Inc. that converts 2D images and videos into immersive 3D experiences using Spatial AI and a Neural Depth Engine. It provides tools for content creators to generate depth maps and create spatial video content for various devices, including traditional screens and XR devices. The platform also includes Switchable-Display Hardware for OEMs to integrate true holographic depth into their devices.
