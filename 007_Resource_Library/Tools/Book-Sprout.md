## Summary
BookSprout is an online platform designed for authors to manage Advance Reader Copies (ARCs) and gather book reviews. It facilitates the distribution of pre-publication books to readers who provide reviews on various retail sites, and it helps automate the review collection process. The platform connects authors with a community of readers willing to provide feedback before or after a book's launch.
