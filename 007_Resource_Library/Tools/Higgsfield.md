## Summary
Higgsfield AI is a generative AI platform that provides tools for creating high-fidelity, controllable, and stylistically consistent video and image content from text and image prompts. It offers a unified, software-defined studio environment for professional video production, leveraging large-scale diffusion models and NVIDIA AI for training and inference. The platform aims to support millions of users simultaneously and includes features for image generation, cinematic video creation, editing, and workflow automation.
