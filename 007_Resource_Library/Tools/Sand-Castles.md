## Summary
Sandcastles AI is a research and scriptwriting platform designed for short-form video creators. It analyzes viral content across Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts, identifies high-performing videos, and helps generate scripts based on their successful structures. The tool provides insights into why videos go viral and assists in creating new content with proven hooks and storytelling principles.
