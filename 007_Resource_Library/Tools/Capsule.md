## Summary
Capsule is an AI-assisted, browser-based video editing tool that helps businesses create on-brand videos at scale. It converts After Effects files into templates, enabling non-creative teams to produce videos while maintaining brand consistency. The platform offers features like VoiceMagic for audio enhancement and Auto Frame for responsive video across platforms.
