## Summary
VideoHooks is an online library providing attention-grabbing 'hook' clips and ideas. It is designed to assist creators in crafting strong video beginnings for advertisements and social media posts, aiming to immediately capture audience attention and prevent scrolling. These hooks are crucial for increasing watch time and engagement on platforms like TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube Shorts.
