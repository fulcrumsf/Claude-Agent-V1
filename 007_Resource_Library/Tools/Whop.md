## Summary
Whop is an American social commerce platform and online marketplace that enables entrepreneurs, influencers, and small businesses to sell digital products, memberships, communities, and software directly to consumers. It provides tools for building customized storefronts, managing subscriptions, and fostering online communities. The platform supports various payment methods and offers features like analytics and integrations with community platforms.
