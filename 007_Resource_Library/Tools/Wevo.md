## Summary
Wevo is an AI-driven UX research and insights platform designed to optimize digital experiences. It combines human-augmented AI with user feedback to provide actionable insights, including visual attention heatmaps, friction scores, and sentiment analysis. The platform helps product, marketing, and design teams prioritize improvements for conversion and engagement by evaluating campaigns and designs with target audiences.
