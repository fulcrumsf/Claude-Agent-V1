## Summary
ClipZap is an AI-powered media generation and editing platform that automates the creation of videos, images, and music. It integrates various leading AI models into a unified workflow editor to streamline content production. The platform enables users to quickly generate and edit short videos, translate content, add subtitles, and perform face-swapping for marketing, social media, and other creative needs.
