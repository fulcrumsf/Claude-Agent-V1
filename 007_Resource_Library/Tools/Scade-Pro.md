## Summary
Scade.pro is a no-code AI platform that enables users to integrate over 1,500 AI models for audio, image, text, and video into their projects. It allows for the creation of AI agents and automated workflows to build and ship functional products without extensive coding. The platform aims to accelerate project timelines, reduce costs, and generate revenue by simplifying AI integration.
