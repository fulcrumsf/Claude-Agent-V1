## Summary
Coolify is an open-source, self-hostable platform that provides an alternative to commercial PaaS solutions like Heroku and Vercel. It allows users to deploy and manage applications, databases, and services on their own servers with full control over their infrastructure. Coolify supports various frameworks, offers one-click deployments for many services, and integrates with Git for streamlined deployments.
