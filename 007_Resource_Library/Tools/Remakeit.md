## Summary
Remakeit.io is an AI-powered SaaS tool that transforms long-form videos, such as YouTube content, or text ideas into short, viral video clips. It automates the process of clipping, adding subtitles, and optimizing content for platforms like TikTok, Reels, and Shorts. The tool aims to help creators generate monetizable short-form video content efficiently.
