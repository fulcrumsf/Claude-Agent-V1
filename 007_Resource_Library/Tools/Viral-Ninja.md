## Summary
Viral Ninja is an AI-powered SaaS platform designed for e-commerce and social commerce, focusing on dropshipping and TikTok Shop. It provides tools for product trend research, including a product database, tracker, market insights, store scraper, and supplier database, to help users identify and analyze trending products.
