## Summary
Tenor is an online GIF search engine and database, owned by Google, that allows users to find and share animated GIFs. It offers a GIF Keyboard for Android, iOS, and macOS, and its technology is integrated into various messaging apps and platforms. Tenor's API, which allowed developers to integrate GIFs into apps, is scheduled to be decommissioned on June 30, 2026.
