## Summary
Lovart AI is an AI design agent that automates the creative process, generating professional visual assets like logos, brand kits, social media graphics, and videos from natural language prompts. It features an infinite canvas workspace for organizing and refining designs and integrates multiple AI models to act as a virtual creative director.
