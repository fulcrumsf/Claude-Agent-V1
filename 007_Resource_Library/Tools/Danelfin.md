## Summary
Danelfin is an AI-powered stock analytics platform that helps investors make data-driven investment decisions. It analyzes over 10,000 features per stock daily to generate an AI Score (1-10), predicting the probability of a stock or ETF outperforming the market in the next three months. The platform provides insights, trade ideas, and portfolio optimization tools for US and European stocks and US-listed ETFs.
