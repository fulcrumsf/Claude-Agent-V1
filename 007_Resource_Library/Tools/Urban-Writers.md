## Summary
The Urban Writers is an online content creation platform and ghostwriting marketplace that connects clients with freelance writers, editors, and designers. It offers services for various content types, including books, articles, SEO content, and provides project management tools. The platform is also known as Dibbly | The Urban Writers.
