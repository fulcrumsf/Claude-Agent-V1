## Summary
Artflow AI is an innovative AI-powered platform designed for digital artists, storytellers, and content creators. It enables users to generate AI-driven characters, create unique animations, and craft engaging narratives by generating images and video-style story content. The platform allows for consistent visuals for comics, shorts, and character-driven narratives without advanced design skills.
