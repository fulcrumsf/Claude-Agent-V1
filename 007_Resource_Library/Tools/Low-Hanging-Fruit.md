## Summary
Lowest Hanging Fruit is a product discovery tool and Chrome extension for Amazon Influencers. It helps users identify products with high commission potential and low competition (fewer existing influencer videos) to prioritize content for their Amazon storefronts. The tool offers advanced product discovery, analytics, and brand scrubbing features.
