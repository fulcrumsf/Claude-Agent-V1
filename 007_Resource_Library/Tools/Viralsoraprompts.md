## Summary
ViralSoraPrompts.com is an online tool designed to generate professional, cinematic prompts for Sora 2 AI video generation. It transforms user ideas into detailed prompts, offering style presets, mood controls, and expert tips to create viral-ready videos. The tool aims to help users craft prompts that result in realistic and engaging AI-generated video content.
