## Summary
Pykaso AI is a generative artificial intelligence platform that provides tools for creating ultra-realistic AI images and videos. It enables users to train custom LoRa AI models for consistent character generation and rapidly produce high-quality content. The platform is designed for content creators, social influencers, and digital marketers to generate and monetize AI characters and multimedia content.
