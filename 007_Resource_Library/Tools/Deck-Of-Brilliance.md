## Summary
Deck of Brilliance is an online toolkit offering over 200 ideation tools, prompts, and frameworks for creative strategy, briefs, and campaign ideation. It provides a structured system to generate stronger concepts, headlines, and storylines for marketing and content projects. A free 'Essential Toolkit' is available upon registration.
