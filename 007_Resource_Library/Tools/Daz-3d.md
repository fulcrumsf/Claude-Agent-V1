## Summary
Daz 3D is a company offering 3D content and software, primarily Daz Studio, a free application for creating and rendering 3D scenes and characters. It provides a platform for users to assemble, pose, and render human and non-human figures, along with an extensive asset store for 3D models, props, and environments.
