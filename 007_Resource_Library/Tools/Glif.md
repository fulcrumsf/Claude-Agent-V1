## Summary
Glif.app is an AI-powered creative agent platform that enables users to generate images, videos, audio, and complete ad campaigns from simple text prompts. It acts as an AI orchestrator, combining multiple AI models to produce various forms of creative content. The platform is designed for creators, marketers, and e-commerce sellers to quickly produce visual and audio assets without extensive manual production.
