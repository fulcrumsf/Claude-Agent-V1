## Summary
Patterned AI is an artificial intelligence platform that specializes in generating unique, royalty-free, and seamless patterns and designs for various applications like textiles, wallpaper, and packaging. It allows users to create patterns from text descriptions, customize parameters like scale and color, and export print-ready high-resolution or vector files. The platform is used by designers and manufacturers to streamline the pattern design workflow.
