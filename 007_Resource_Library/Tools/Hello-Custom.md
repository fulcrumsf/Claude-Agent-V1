## Summary
HelloCustom is a personalization automation tool designed for Etsy sellers to automatically personalize product graphics for print-on-demand (POD) items. It integrates with print providers to streamline order fulfillment, making the process of selling personalized products more hands-off. The tool aims to simplify the creation and management of customizable products without requiring extensive design or technical skills.
