## Summary
Ossa.ai is an AI-powered platform designed to create faceless, short-form videos from text scripts for social media platforms like YouTube, TikTok, and Instagram. It automates video generation, including AI voiceovers and automatic subtitles, to simplify content creation without requiring editing skills or appearing on camera. The tool helps creators produce unique, engaging videos quickly and efficiently.
