## Summary
Marketer.com is an AI-powered marketing platform designed for e-commerce teams to manage and optimize their advertising campaigns. It automates ad creation, audience targeting, and performance optimization to increase return on ad spend (ROAS) and streamline marketing workflows. The platform is built for various e-commerce brands, including those on Shopify.
