## Summary
Books.by is a direct-to-reader publishing platform that allows authors to create their own print-on-demand bookstores. Authors can sell books directly to readers, control pricing, and retain 100% of their royalties by paying a flat annual fee, with the platform handling printing, shipping, and payments.
