## Summary
Pippit by CapCut is an AI-powered marketing creative platform that generates videos, posters, and other marketing assets from a single photo, link, or prompt. It is designed for marketers and businesses to create consistent, on-brand content across various social media platforms. The tool automates content production, allowing users to transform images or product links into scroll-stopping videos and matching posters without extensive editing skills.
