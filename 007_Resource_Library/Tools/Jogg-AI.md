## Summary
JoggAI is an AI video creation platform that enables users to generate dynamic video ads and other content using AI avatars, automated scriptwriting, and text-to-video capabilities. It allows users to transform text, images, product URLs, or existing media into polished videos with realistic digital presenters and generated voices. The platform aims to simplify video production by eliminating the need for traditional filming, editing software, or actors.
