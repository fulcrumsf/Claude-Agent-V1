## Summary
PLR.me is an online marketplace providing private label rights (PLR) content and templates, primarily for coaches, consultants, and online business owners in various niches, including wellness. It offers a library of editable articles, workbooks, courses, and marketing assets that users can rebrand and use for their products or lead magnets. The platform also includes AI-powered tools to assist with content personalization and repurposing, operating on a credit-based system.
