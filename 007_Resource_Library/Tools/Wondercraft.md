## Summary
Wondercraft is an AI-powered platform that transforms text, URLs, or documents into narrated audio, podcasts, ads, and video content. It offers features like AI voice generation, voice cloning, AI scripting, and a timeline editor for producing polished audio and video without manual recording. The platform is designed for teams and creators to produce content at scale.
