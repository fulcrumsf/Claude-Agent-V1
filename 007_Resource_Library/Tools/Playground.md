## Summary
Playground AI is a web-based platform that enables users to generate and edit images and graphics using artificial intelligence. It offers text-to-image generation, templates, and editing features like background removal and upscaling. The tool aims to make graphic design accessible for various purposes, including social media, marketing, and personal projects.
