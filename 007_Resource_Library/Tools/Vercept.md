## Summary
Vercept developed Vy, a Mac-native AI desktop agent that used natural language commands and visual understanding to automate workflows across desktop applications. It could perform tasks like data entry and file organization by understanding the screen and taking actions. Vercept was acquired by Anthropic in February 2026, and its standalone product is being wound down as its technology is integrated into Anthropic's Claude to enhance its computer use capabilities.
