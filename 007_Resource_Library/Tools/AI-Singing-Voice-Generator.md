## This is yours, not ours.

Most AI tools hand you a finished song. Controlla starts with your voice and lets you put it anywhere.

### Your voice, anywhere.

Country ballad, hip-hop record, slow indie build. Same voice, different rooms.

### 20 seconds is enough.

Talk, hum, or sing. Controlla picks up how you sound from there.

### No studio needed.

Just your phone, a quiet room, and an idea.

Built for you

### If you're producing, this drops into your session like real vocals.

Most AI voice tools come back over-processed. Controlla preserves the take so you can mix it yourself.

### Dynamics intact.

Quiet phrases stay quiet. Loud ones stay loud. No baked-in compression.

### You own the mix.

Apply your own EQ and compression. The vocal does what you want in your song.

### Performance preserved.

Vibrato, breath, phrasing. The take you uploaded is the take that comes back.
