## Summary
Viralfindr is a web-based SaaS tool designed for Instagram content research and analysis. It allows users to identify trending content, analyze competitor performance by viral score, and discover viral niches to inform their content strategy. The platform offers features like searching by keywords, hashtags, or profiles and provides insights into top-performing posts.
