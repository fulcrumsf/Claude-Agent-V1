## Summary
Class Central is an online search engine and aggregator for Massive Open Online Courses (MOOCs) and other online courses from various universities and providers. It allows users to find free or paid courses, compare syllabi, read reviews, and track their learning progress. The platform lists over 250,000 courses, including many with free certificates, from institutions like Harvard, Stanford, MIT, Google, and IBM.
