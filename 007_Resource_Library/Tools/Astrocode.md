## Summary
Astrocade is an AI-powered platform that enables users to create playable browser games from text prompts without coding or design skills. It facilitates rapid prototyping of game ideas and offers a social platform for playing, sharing, and remixing user-generated games. The platform automates the generation of art, animation, sound, and gameplay mechanics.
