## Summary
MagicLight.AI is an AI video platform specializing in generating long-form narrative videos, capable of producing content up to 50 minutes in length. It allows creators to transform scripts, text, or images into animated and cinematic stories, ensuring consistency in characters and scenes. The platform integrates features such as script generation, character creation, storyboarding, AI animation, voiceovers, and subtitles.
