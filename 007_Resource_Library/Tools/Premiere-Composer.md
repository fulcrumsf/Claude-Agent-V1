## Summary
Mister Horse Premiere Composer, now known as Animation Composer, is a plugin for Adobe Premiere Pro that provides a library of drag-and-drop motion graphics presets. It allows users to quickly add titles, transitions, and animations to their video projects without creating them from scratch.
