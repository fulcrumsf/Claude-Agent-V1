## Summary
AudioX is an AI-powered platform that generates audio, music, video, and images from various inputs such as text, images, and videos. It offers features like text-to-audio, image-to-audio, and video-to-music to assist content creators in producing professional-quality media. The platform aims to simplify content creation by providing multi-modal AI input and smart editing tools.
