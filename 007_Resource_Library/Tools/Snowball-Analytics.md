## Summary
Snowball is an AI-powered SaaS tool designed for social media content creators and marketers. It analyzes social media content, particularly on platforms like TikTok, to identify viral trends, content patterns, and performance insights. The tool helps users develop data-backed content strategies and streamline their content creation workflow.
