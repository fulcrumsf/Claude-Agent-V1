## Summary
BuildThatIdea is a no-code web-based platform that enables users to create, launch, and monetize custom AI applications, also known as AI agents. Users can define their AI app's functionality, select from various large language models, and integrate a knowledge base without writing any code. The platform offers one-click deployment and monetization options, catering to creators, indie hackers, and businesses looking to develop AI-powered products.
