## Summary
EtsyCheck was an all-in-one toolkit designed for Etsy sellers to optimize listings, master SEO, and increase sales. It offered features such as shop and listing analysis, keyword trend analysis, and tag generation. In May 2025, EtsyCheck officially rebranded to RankHero to reflect an expanded vision and address trademark concerns.
