## Summary
Overlap is an AI clipping and social distribution platform that automates the transformation of long-form video and audio content into short, branded clips for social media. It utilizes multimodal AI agents to identify key moments, apply brand guidelines, and publish across various social platforms. The tool is designed for high-volume media companies, creators, and marketing teams to scale their content strategy.
