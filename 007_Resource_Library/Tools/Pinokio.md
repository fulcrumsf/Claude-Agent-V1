## Summary
Pinokio is a free, open-source desktop application that acts as an AI browser and one-click launcher for local AI tools and open-source projects. It simplifies the installation, running, and management of various AI applications, such as image generators, large language models, and audio tools, on Mac, Windows, and Linux. The tool automates complex setup processes, allowing users to run AI models locally without requiring command-line expertise, prioritizing privacy and local operation.
