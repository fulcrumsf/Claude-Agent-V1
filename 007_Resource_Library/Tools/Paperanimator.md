## Summary
PaperAnimator is an online platform that transforms static images into animated graphics with a paper-cut or stop-motion style. It offers features like paper cut-out effects, fold-out animations, and text-based animations. Users can customize effects, remove backgrounds, and export animations as MP4s (with green screen option) or transparent PNGs.
