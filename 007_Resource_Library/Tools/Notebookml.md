## Summary
NotebookLM, now rebranded as Gemini Notebook, is an AI-powered research and content organization tool developed by Google. It allows users to upload various sources like PDFs, websites, and videos, then summarizes content, answers questions, and generates study aids or audio overviews based on these materials. The tool is designed to assist with research, learning, and organizing complex information.
