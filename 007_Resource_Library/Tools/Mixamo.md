## Summary
Mixamo is an Adobe-owned online platform that offers services for 3D character animation, including automatic rigging and a vast library of motion-captured animations. Users can upload their own 3D humanoid characters (FBX, OBJ, or ZIP files) to be automatically rigged and then apply pre-made animations, which can be downloaded for use in games, films, and other digital media projects. It simplifies the animation process by providing ready-to-use character rigs and animations.
