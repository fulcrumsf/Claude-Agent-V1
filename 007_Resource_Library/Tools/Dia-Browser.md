## Summary
Dia Browser is a Chromium-based desktop application developed by The Browser Company, featuring integrated AI capabilities. It allows users to summarize content, answer questions across open tabs, and automate research and planning tasks. The browser utilizes built-in AI agents to understand web context and interact with various online tools.
