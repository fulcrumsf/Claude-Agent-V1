## Summary
Vidu AI is an AI video generator that converts text and images into high-quality videos. It offers features like Text-to-Video, Image-to-Video, and Reference-to-Video, enabling users to create animations and maintain consistent character appearance across videos. The tool is designed for creators, marketers, and teams to streamline video production.
