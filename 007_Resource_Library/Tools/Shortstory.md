## Summary
StoryShort AI is an AI-powered platform designed to automate the creation of faceless short-form videos for social media platforms such as TikTok and YouTube Shorts. It generates videos from text or prompts, including AI-written scripts, voiceovers, and captions. The tool also provides features for auto-publishing content to connected social media accounts.
