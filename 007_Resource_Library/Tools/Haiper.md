## Summary
Haiper AI is a visual-generation platform that allows users to create short videos from text or images, transform existing clips, and generate still images. It provides text-to-video, image-to-video, keyframe conditioning, and video-to-video generation capabilities through its web and iOS applications, API, and ComfyUI integration. The platform is designed for creators and product teams to generate professional-grade video content.
