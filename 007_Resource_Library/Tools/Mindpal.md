## Summary
MindPal is a no-code platform designed for building AI agents and multi-agent workflows. It enables users to train AI on their expertise, frameworks, and standard operating procedures to create AI-powered support, guided implementations, and content tools. These AI agents and workflows can then be deployed as chatbots, embedded tools, or branded portals.
