## Summary
HautechAI is a generative AI platform designed for commerce and marketing teams, particularly in fashion. It enables the creation of photorealistic product visuals, including on-model images, virtual try-ons, and AI product photography, to scale content production for ads and e-commerce. The platform operates on a credit-based system, offering various AI solutions for visual content generation.
