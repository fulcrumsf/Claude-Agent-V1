## Summary
There's An AI For That (TAAFT) is the world's largest AI tools marketplace and directory. It helps users discover and compare thousands of AI-powered products across various categories and use cases, offering features like AI-driven search, trending tools, and exclusive deals. The platform also provides a daily newsletter to keep users updated on new AI launches, news, and models.
