## Summary
Make.com (formerly Integromat) is a visual, low-code automation platform that enables users to build workflows, called scenarios, by connecting over 3,000 applications through a drag-and-drop interface. It allows for the automation of multi-step processes and data flow between applications without coding. The platform also integrates AI capabilities, including AI agents and connections to various AI apps, to enhance workflow intelligence and decision-making.
