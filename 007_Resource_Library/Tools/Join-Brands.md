## Summary
JoinBrands is a user-generated content (UGC) and creator marketplace that connects brands with content creators for producing authentic videos, photos, and social media posts. The platform allows brands to post content jobs, review creator applications, manage deliverables, and handle payments for campaigns across platforms like TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, and Amazon. It streamlines the process of acquiring UGC for marketing purposes, offering features like content approval, usage rights, and built-in Spark Ad codes for TikTok.
