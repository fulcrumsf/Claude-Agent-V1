## Summary
Kortex is an AI-powered 'second brain' and note-taking application designed for creators and knowledge workers to capture, organize, and synthesize ideas, notes, and highlights. It features a Markdown editor, pane navigation, document management with folders and tags, and integrations with other tools like Readwise. The platform aims to streamline workflows for writing and content creation.
