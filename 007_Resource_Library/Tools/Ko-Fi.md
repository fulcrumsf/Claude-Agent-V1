## Summary
Ko-fi is an online platform that enables creators to receive financial support from their audience. It facilitates one-time tips, recurring memberships, commissions, and the sale of digital and physical products. The platform offers a free tier with 0% fees on tips and a 5% fee on other transactions, with a paid Ko-fi Gold option to eliminate platform fees.
