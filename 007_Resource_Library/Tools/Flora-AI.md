## Summary
FloraFauna AI is a browser-based generative creative suite that provides a node-based visual canvas for unifying over 50 AI models for text, image, video, and audio generation. It enables professionals to generate and edit various media, facilitating structured and scalable creative workflows. The platform aims to streamline the creative process by allowing users to build evolving networks of ideas and create repeatable, reference-guided systems.
