## Summary
Syllaby.io is an AI-powered platform designed to streamline video content creation for social media. It enables users to generate video scripts, create faceless videos, and produce videos with AI avatars or cloned voices. The platform also assists with content ideation, scheduling, and provides analytics for social media marketing.
