## Summary
Surge AI is an American multinational data annotation company that provides human feedback for training AI models, particularly in reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF) and language data annotation. It helps tech companies like Google and Microsoft build smarter AI by supplying data that helps models understand nuance.
