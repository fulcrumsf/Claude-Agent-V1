## Summary
Zendrop is a dropshipping platform designed for e-commerce sellers to source products from vetted suppliers, primarily in the US and globally, and automate order fulfillment. It integrates with platforms like Shopify to streamline product imports, manage inventory, and handle shipping with options for faster delivery and custom branding. The platform also offers features such as private labeling and order tracking to help scale dropshipping businesses.
