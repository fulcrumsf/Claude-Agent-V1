## Summary
This Civitai link is for a Stable Diffusion XL (SDXL) LoRA called 'Painting Style Lora SDXL (Milde style)'. It is an AI model designed to apply a specific 'Milde style' painting aesthetic to images generated using Stable Diffusion XL. The LoRA influences the texture of the canvas, background, and brush strokes, and can add a painterly style to checkpoints that may not inherently support it.
