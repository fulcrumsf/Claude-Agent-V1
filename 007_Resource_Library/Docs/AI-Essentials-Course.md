AI Essentials:
Building Foundational
Knowledge

Important definition to keep in mind

Artificial
Intelligence (AI)

Technology that enables machines to
perform tasks that typically require human
intelligence, such as reasoning, learning,
or problem-solving.

Machine
Learning (ML)

A subset of AI where systems learn from data
and improve their performance over time
without being explicitly programmed.

Generative AI
AI that creates new content, like text, images,
audio, or code, based on patterns it
has learned from data.

Large Language
Models (LLMs)

Advanced AI models trained on vast amounts
of text to understand and generate human-
like language.

Agentic AI
Autonomous systems that use Machine
Learning (ML), Gen AI, and Natural Language
Processing (NLP) to achieve complex goals
without constant human guidance.

How Large Language Models are built

Training stage

Learning the language

The model studies billions of words

from books, websites, and articles.

It doesn’t memorize text, it learns

patterns in grammar, facts, and

relationships between words.

Using self-supervised learning, it

predicts the next word in a

sentence, over and over,

adjusting when it’s wrong

and reinforcing when it’s right.

01

This process builds a foundational model that understands
language structure.

Tuning stage

Shaping behavior

At this point, the foundational

model knows language but not

how to behave.

Builders use smaller, curated

datasets to refine its responses

and purpose. There are two parts

to the tuning stage:

Instruction tuning:

Teaches the model to

follow directions and

respond conversationally.

Behavioral alignment:

Human reviewers guide tone

and personality (helpful,

creative, technical, etc.).

02

Implementation
stage

Putting it to work

The tuned model is connected to

real-world data and applications.

Organizations tailor it with:

Their knowledge base for
accurate product or
company insights.

Industry-specific data for
relevance.

Customer or workflow
context for precision.

03

Fine-tune model

Provide model with

workflows, or rules

Key value drivers of Generative AI

Automation

AI can understand
natural language and
automate complex,
time-consuming
tasks once thought
impossible to
standardize.

Personalization
and content
creation

Generative AI
creates personalized
content, enhancing
experiences when
done right, but
feeling generic if
done wrong.

Enhanced
productivity

AI combines
automation and
content creation to
boost efficiency,
handling tasks so
teams can focus on
strategy.

Generative AI’s true value lies in combining automation,
personalization, and productivity, creating systems that
think, act, and adapt alongside you.

Data-first + AI-first mindset

Data-first mindset

Treat data as the core
strategic asset

Prioritise accuracy, quality,
and governance

Validate: Does this data
reflect reality?

Make data usable for AI
before building the agent

AI-first mindset

Ask: Is AI the right solution
for this problem?

Confirm the dataset
supports the use case

Apply AI to enhance
efficiency or decision-making

Build agents only after a strong
Data-First foundation

Never trust an AI output that includes facts, figures, dates,

or citations without independent verification.
