## Uses of Trang Vi Khang Medicine

> [**Trang Vi Khang**](https://nhathuocthanthien.com.vn/san-pham/trang-vi-khang/) is a traditional Chinese medicine that helps regulate the intestines, stabilize bowel movements, and restore digestive function. It has antibacterial and anti-inflammatory properties, eliminating harmful bacteria in the intestines. It regulates gastric acid levels, limiting ulcers, effectively reducing pain, and preventing intestinal spasms.

## How to use Trang Vi Khang medicine

Adults: Take 1 sachet three times a day.

Children: take ½ sachet three times a day.

Mix the product with warm water and drink 15 minutes before meals.

## Target users of Trang Vi Khang medicine

People with foodborne infections or poisonings.

People with [acute and chronic](https://nhathuocthanthien.com.vn/benh/viem-dai-trang/) [**colitis**](https://nhathuocthanthien.com.vn/benh/viem-dai-trang/).
