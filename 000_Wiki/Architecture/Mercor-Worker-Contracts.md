# Mercor Worker Contracts

## What It Is
These are the two onboarding contracts Tony uses for Mercor: the main worker agreement and the worker invention assignment agreement. They define the working relationship, the service expectations, the IP assignment terms, and the baseline constraints around Mercor work.

## Key Concepts
- Worker relationship and service scope
- Non-circumvention and client restrictions
- Fees, payment, and performance expectations
- IP assignment and invention ownership
- Past/future service coverage and continued obligation language

## How Tony Uses This
Use these contracts as reference when Tony asks about Mercor onboarding, worker obligations, or IP-related terms. They are kept together in `009_AI_Jobs/Mercor/` as the canonical source files.
