# Vmake UGC Video Generator

## What It Is
Vmake is an all-in-one UGC video workflow that combines generation, editing, enhancement, and optimization. It is aimed at creators, sellers, affiliates, and local businesses that need faster short-form production.

- One image or idea can become a finished UGC video
- Generation, cleanup, captions, and enhancement are all in one system
- The tool is optimized for TikTok, Reels, Shorts, Shopify, and Amazon Inspire
- Supports product showcases, avatars, watermark removal, background removal, and more
