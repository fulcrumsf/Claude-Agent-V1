# Stock Video AI Training Marketplace

## What It Is
This is a creator marketplace for contributing content to AI training and stock media projects. The pitch is simple: creators can earn money by supplying approved photos, videos, illustrations, and 3D work to AI labs.

## Key Concepts
- Paid creator contributions
- AI-lab demand for licensed content
- Wide support for photography, video, design, and 3D
- Monthly payouts and consent-based projects

## How Tony Uses This
Use this when Tony wants to think about content as a monetizable asset class beyond direct audience monetization. It is a useful reference for creator-side revenue models in the AI era.
