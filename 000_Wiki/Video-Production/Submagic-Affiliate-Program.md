# Submagic Affiliate Program

## What It Is
Submagic is positioned as a short-form video editor with a strong affiliate program. The source emphasizes recurring commissions, fast onboarding, and creator-focused social proof.

## Key Concepts
- Built around fast short-form editing
- Affiliate pitch is part of the product story
- Focuses on creators, coaches, influencers, and content sites
- The marketing angle is "one video into multiple shorts"

## How Tony Uses This
Use this when Tony wants to evaluate or promote captioning and short-form editing tools that are already optimized for affiliate distribution. It also works as a market signal for the broader short-form tooling stack.
