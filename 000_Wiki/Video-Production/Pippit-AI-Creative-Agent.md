# Pippit AI Creative Agent

## What It Is
Pippit is a CapCut/ByteDance-oriented creative agent for generating images and videos from prompts and reference assets. The source emphasizes a simple workflow, multiple templates, and tight fit with TikTok ecosystem formats.

## Key Concepts
- Supports video and image generation from prompts and reference files
- Positioned around TikTok, TikTok Shop, CapCut, and ads
- Template-driven creation can accelerate repeatable content formats
- Designed to create social posts, product posters, and similar assets

## How Tony Uses This
Use this when Tony needs a fast creative-agent workflow for TikTok-style content or short-form promotional assets. It is especially relevant if the goal is quick iteration over polished control.
