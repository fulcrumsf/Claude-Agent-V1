# Short Form Video Creation Stack

## What It Is
This is the stack pattern behind modern short-form video production: one tool for generation, one for clipping and repurposing, and one for captioning and polish. The source notes point to Vmake-style UGC generation, OpusClip-style repackaging, and Submagic-style finishing.

## Key Concepts
- One image or idea can become a full UGC ad or short-form asset
- Generation, enhancement, and optimization can live in the same workflow
- Clipping and captions are part of the production stack, not separate work
- The stack is designed for creators, e-commerce, affiliate marketing, and local business promos
- Affiliate programs matter because the tools are also distribution channels

## How Tony Uses This
Use this when Tony wants to compare or assemble a short-form video stack for growth work. It is useful for deciding which tool handles generation, which handles clipping, and which handles polish.
