# Pipeline Orchestration

## What It Is
This is a bundle of creator-side orchestration notes for building story-driven AI video pipelines. It covers a general viral video framework, a creator knowledge-to-product pipeline, and a Neon Parcel-specific channel pipeline.

## Key Concepts
- Interviewing the creator conversationally
- Turning rough ideas into structured production specs
- Retention and pacing frameworks
- Knowledge products as a related but distinct pipeline
- Channel-specific orchestration for Neon Parcel

Use this as the reference page when Tony is building or refining video pipeline logic, especially for story structure, content-gap analysis, and channel-specific orchestration. The source notes stay together in `001_Architecture/Tools/Video-Generation/Pipeline_Docs/`.
