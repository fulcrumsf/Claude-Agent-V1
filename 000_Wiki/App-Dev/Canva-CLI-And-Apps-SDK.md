# Canva CLI and Apps SDK

## What It Is
This is the developer surface for building and managing Canva apps with `@canva/cli` and the Apps SDK. The documentation covers intents, design editor integration, data connectors, auth, testing, monetization, and deployment.

## Key Concepts
- `@canva/cli` is the command-line entry point for creating and testing apps
- Intents define the app's user-facing actions
- Data connectors and URL expanders make Canva app behavior more dynamic
- The docs include auth, localization, security, and monetization flows
- The AI-agents tutorial shows that CLI-based onboarding works well for agentic automation

## How Tony Uses This
Use this when Tony wants to automate Canva workflows or understand the app surface for building design tools. It is also relevant when comparing MCP-style integrations to CLI-first automation.
