# TikTok Shop Affiliate Compliance

## What It Is
This is the compliance reference bundle for TikTok Shop creator campaigns, promotional content rules, prohibited products, misleading-content guidance, beauty and skincare restrictions, weight-management guidance, customer service/order policy, and platform growth practices. It is the policy set Tony should check before creating or revising TikTok Shop affiliate videos.

## Key Concepts
- Creator campaigns require compliance with TikTok Terms of Service, Community Guidelines, Advertising Policies, and campaign-specific program materials
- TikTok Shop short videos and livestreams must stay closed-loop and keep users on-platform for purchase flows
- Promotional content must be accurate, non-misleading, and aligned with the product detail page and product packaging
- Paid promotion disclosures such as `#ad` or `#sponsored` must be clear and conspicuous when required — **except** for TikTok Shop Creator affiliate content specifically: attaching a TikTok Shop product link auto-adds a "Creator earns commission" tag, which itself serves as the disclosure (confirmed by Tony's direct platform observation, 2026-07-12 — not documented in the local TOS bundle text). For that content type, use relevant hashtags instead of `#ad`/`#sponsored`.
- Prohibited products, deceptive discounts, unsupported claims, and exaggerated results are high-risk areas
- Nudity and sexualized content are restricted even when used as a hook or transition — see the dedicated nudity/sexualized-content guide
- Sellers are accountable for order cancellation, return, and refund handling and for how review requests are worded (no incentivized or coercive review asks)
- Repeated or severe policy violations trigger TikTok's Seller Enforcement Policy — escalating penalties up to shop suspension
- The LIVE Growth Playbook covers platform-level growth mechanics for TikTok Shop LIVE, separate from short-video promotional rules

## How Tony Uses This
Use this as the policy reference when Tony asks whether a TikTok Shop video, caption, voiceover, or product claim is allowed. It is the first stop for checking whether the affiliate video can be posted as written or needs to be rewritten to match TikTok's rules.
