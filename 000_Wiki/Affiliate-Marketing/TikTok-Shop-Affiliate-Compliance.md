# TikTok Shop Affiliate Compliance

## What It Is
This is the compliance reference bundle for TikTok Shop creator campaigns, promotional content rules, prohibited products, misleading-content guidance, beauty and skincare restrictions, and weight-management guidance. It is the policy set Tony should check before creating or revising TikTok Shop affiliate videos.

## Key Concepts
- Creator campaigns require compliance with TikTok Terms of Service, Community Guidelines, Advertising Policies, and campaign-specific program materials
- TikTok Shop short videos and livestreams must stay closed-loop and keep users on-platform for purchase flows
- Promotional content must be accurate, non-misleading, and aligned with the product detail page and product packaging
- Paid promotion disclosures such as `#ad` or `#sponsored` must be clear and conspicuous when required
- Prohibited products, deceptive discounts, unsupported claims, and exaggerated results are high-risk areas

## How Tony Uses This
Use this as the policy reference when Tony asks whether a TikTok Shop video, caption, voiceover, or product claim is allowed. It is the first stop for checking whether the affiliate video can be posted as written or needs to be rewritten to match TikTok's rules.
