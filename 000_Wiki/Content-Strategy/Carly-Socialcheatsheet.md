# Carly Socialcheatsheet

## What It Is
This source looks like a creator-economy storefront centered on digital products, AI creator offers, PLR bundles, and resale-rights products. The emphasis is on selling creator-facing products across multiple niches.

## Key Concepts
- Digital products are the core monetization format
- PLR, MRR, and resale rights are repeatedly used as hooks
- The offer stack spans Pinterest, POD, AI avatars, and faceless marketing
- Social channels are used as traffic sources for productized info offers

## How Tony Uses This
Use this when Tony wants to evaluate creator-economy product ladders or build offer stacks around digital products and social traffic. It is a useful example of how multiple low-ticket offers can be layered.
