# ProfitTree Etsy Research

## What It Is
ProfitTree is an Etsy product research and profit-tracking tool that surfaces live demand signals, competitor context, and profitability data for sellers. The page is built around reducing guesswork in product decisions.

## Key Concepts
- Real-time demand and competitor visibility
- Profit tracking and order breakdowns
- Social proof aimed at serious Etsy sellers
- Useful for POD and handmade product analysis

## How Tony Uses This
Use this when Tony wants to assess Etsy opportunities or understand a product's margins and market traction before committing inventory or ad spend. It is a practical reference for e-commerce decision support.
