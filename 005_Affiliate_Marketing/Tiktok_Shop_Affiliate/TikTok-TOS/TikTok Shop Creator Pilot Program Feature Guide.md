### How It Works

The Creator Pilot Program is designed to guide TikTok creators with 1,000 to 5,000 followers on their journey to becoming successful TikTok Shop sellers. It's all about helping you learn the ropes, create amazing shoppable content, and grow your influence!  
1. **Sign up for the journey:** If you're a TikTok creator with 1,000 to 5,000 followers, you can apply for the Creator Pilot Program through our usual entrances like Creator Search Insights, TikTok Studio (look for TikTok Shop for Creator under Monetization), product pages, ShopTab, and the desktop Creator Portal. Because of your follower count, your sign-up page will be tailored specifically for the Pilot Program.
2. **Start with support (and a few gentle limits!)**: Once you've successfully verified your identity and applied for affiliate permission, you'll automatically be enrolled in the program for 30 days. During this time, we'll give you a supportive environment to learn and grow responsibly. This means you'll have a few initial limits (note, these limits are subject to change):
	1. **Product Access**: You'll have access to products on the TikTok Shop Product Marketplace with a shop performance rating of at least 95%.
		2. **Content Posting**: You can post a maximum of 5 Shoppable Videos and 3 Shoppable LIVEs each week.
		3. **Campaigns**: You won't be eligible to join campaigns just yet.
3. **Level up and graduate**: Your goal is to graduate from the program within 30 days, which unlocks more features for you. There's a few ways to think about graduation:
	1. **Grow your following:** If your follower count goes beyond 5,000 during the program, you'll automatically lift restrictions and unlock full posting rights.
		2. **Hit your milestones:** Pay attention to the specific tasks on the Creator Pilot Program page and ensure you hit them within a 30 day timeline in order to unlock unlimited Shoppable LIVEs. If you can't, some of the tasks will reset and you'll have another 30 days to try again.
Your weekly content quota won't change until you grow your following or hit your milestones.  
4. **Stay in the Loop**: Throughout your pilot journey, you can always check your progress on the Pilot Program page, accessible from your Account Settings. We'll also send you in-app messages to guide you during content creation and direct you back to this page if you're nearing your limits.

### Tips

- **Quality over quantity**: While there are limits on your posting frequency, focus on creating high-quality, engaging content.
- **Know our policies**: New creators sometimes face challenges with understanding our policies. Take advantage of the educational resources provided in the program to understand our guidelines and avoid violations. This helps ensure your growth on a strong, stable, and compliant foundation.
- **Experiment with products:** You don't need free samples to start! Explore options like refundable samples or using the search-by-image feature to link products you already love and use at home. As you grow and once you make your first sale, you'll unlock free sample requests too.
- **Engage with the community**: TikTok Shop is all about connection! Create authentic content that converts by showcasing products you truly love.
- **Keep an eye on your health rating**: Your Creator Health Rating is important for graduation. Make sure you're consistently meeting the requirements to keep it healthy!
