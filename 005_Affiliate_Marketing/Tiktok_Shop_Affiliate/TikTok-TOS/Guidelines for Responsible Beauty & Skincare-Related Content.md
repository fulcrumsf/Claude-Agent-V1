## Best Practices:

![image](https://p16-oec-university-ttp-sign.tiktokcdn-us.com/tos-useast5-i-fsvlofm1vi-tx/b9b8aa67061e4a0496652f855adbcd7f~tplv-fsvlofm1vi-image.png?lk3s=5d1a069b&x-expires=2049087381&x-signature=9CMbUs4kZjAOEEgc45hQ4izBGnM%3D) **Keep Product Statements Accurate and Reliable** Product attribute claims must be consistent with what’s shown on the product listing page. Examples of acceptable claims include:  
- Content says lotion is made in America, which is reflected on the lotion packaging.
- Content shows step by step usage of a retinol with information on how much to use based on the instruction found in the product packaging

When featuring makeup or skincare products, apply the product visibly to your face or body in your videos. Alternatively, showcase the product in its packaging. Ensure that your viewers can clearly see the effects of the product on your skin!

**Incorporate Skincare into Your Wellness Routine**

Show how incorporating skincare into daily habits can promote relaxation and self-care. However, do not make exaggerated claims about product benefits, or suggest that skincare products can cure or prevent medical conditions that require professional treatment, such as eczema, depression, or ADHD.

**Qualify Your Reviews with Disclaimers** If your content includes before-and-after comparisons or makes claims about when a product will show results, alert viewers that outcomes may vary and that a product may not produce the same results for them as it did for you. Include phrases such as:  
- *"Results may vary depending on your skin sensitivity"*
- *"This is Day 1 of my acne before using this supplement. This is Day 10 of my acne after using this supplement. "*

Be careful not to include any [**medical claims**](https://seller-us.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=4545471832983342&role=1&course_type=1&from=search&identity=1&anchor_link=EB7F00D0 "https://seller-us.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=4545471832983342&role=1&course_type=1&from=search&identity=1&anchor_link=EB7F00D0") in your content. Adding disclaimers there may not be enough to verify those statements.

**Share what you love about the product** Viewers value hearing your insights and personal experiences with a product. Focus on one brand per piece of content. If you compare products from different brands or describe a product as the “best,” ensure your claims are based on your own experience or supported by credible evidence.  
- *"This hand lotion is my favorite so far. It helps with my dry skin better than X, Y and Z brands."*
- *"I prefer this X brand to Y brand. X brand's line fits better with my personal style, so I always get my clothes from them."*

## What to Avoid:

**Avoid Promoting Prohibited Beauty Products**

Do not promote products that are intended for use by medical professionals or under medical supervision. These include:

- Products with Tretinoin as an ingredient
- Products with Cannabis ingredients
- Corrective or modificative medical devices
- Medical devices for treatment
Products that claim to bleach, whiten, lighten or reduce overall melanin in the skin are also not allowed to be promoted as they can lead to unsafe consumption and false expectations.  
**Avoid Using a Beauty Filter** Keep it real! Do not use digitally generated effects (such as filters or video edits) when promoting skincare products. These can exaggerate results and make the product seem more effective than it really is, even if you include a disclaimer.  
- A creator uses a beauty filter for fuller lips: *"This is the result of using our lip oil. Some say it works like magic."*
- A creator duets a video with a beauty filter: *"This serum absorbs 1000x its weight in water to plump up your skin."*
**Avoid Posting Graphic Close-ups of Skincare or Bodycare Procedures** Graphic close-ups of skincare or bodycare procedures may cause discomfort or disgust to viewers. This includes, but is not limited to, content showing the removal of bodily fluids, skin, or dirt, such as:  
- Pimple and blackhead extraction
- Earwax removal, tongue-scraping, skin-peeling
- Waxing
- Blood extraction
- Product application on intimate body parts when promoting a product.
