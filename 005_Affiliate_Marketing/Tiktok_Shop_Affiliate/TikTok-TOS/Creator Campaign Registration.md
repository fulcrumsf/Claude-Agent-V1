### Summary

**What are the eligibility requirements for creators to participate in TikTok Shop campaigns?**

- Creators must have a Promotion Performance Score of 3.5+ (for affiliates), over 150 points in Creator Health Rating, and fewer than 6 violations in key policies within 30 days. Compliance with TikTok Shop Content Policy and Creator Guidelines is mandatory.

**How is the Promotion Performance Score determined and what activities influence it?**

- The score is based on achieving at least one of these in the last 90 days: 30+ delivered orders in a product category, 20+ posted videos, or 5+ hosted livestreams. This score reflects creator engagement and effectiveness in promotion.

**What are the benefits of participating in Black Friday and Cyber Monday (BFCM) TikTok Shop campaigns?**

- Creators gain increased visibility, TikTok Shopping Ads support, and extra marketing opportunities. Higher historical livestream GMV can unlock greater promotional benefits, including priority placement and ad optimization support.

**What are the key livestream requirements and how do they affect campaign benefits?**

- Eligible creators are auto-approved as Creator Key Livestreamers. A minimum 2-hour livestream is recommended for better ad optimization. Higher past GMV leads to enhanced campaign benefits like Shopping ads and live page visibility.

**What is the process and timeline for campaign application review and withdrawal?**

- Campaign applications typically take 1–3 business days to review. Creators can withdraw by clicking the Cancel button if available; otherwise, they must contact their Partner Manager for assistance.

## What is required for me to have a Promotion Performance Score?

The Promotion Performance Score reflects the quality and trust that customers have in your promotions.  
This dynamic scoring system from 0 (poor) to 5 (excellent) updates daily. It is only available to affiliate creators who have met **at least one** of the following criteria within the **last 30 days**:  
- **5+ delivered orders**, or
- **5+ videos posted**, or
- **2+ livestreams hosted**
For more information on Promotion Performance Score and tips to improve your score, check out our [**Guide to Promotion Performance Score**](https://seller-us.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=3636905400911658&role=1&identity=1 "https://seller-us.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=3636905400911658&role=1&identity=1")**.**

- Eligible Creators will be **automatically approved to go live** once you register as a **Creator Key Livestreamer**.
- The **higher your historical livestream GMV**, the **greater the benefits** TikTok Shop may offer to help you achieve an even higher GMV during the campaign. Benefits may include TikTok Shopping ads, visibility in the Live Shopping section of the landing page, and additional marketing visibility.
- Please ensure you **provide content authorization** via the **TikTok App – Shopping Ads Official** to be eligible for campaign benefits.
- A **minimum livestream duration of 2 hours** is **recommended** for better ad optimization.
- Creators who do not meet the Key Livestream criteria will see an **“Ineligible”** status displayed on the LIVE and Short Video Campaign criteria page.
