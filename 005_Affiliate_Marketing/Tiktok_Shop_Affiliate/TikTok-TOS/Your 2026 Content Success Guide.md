## Respect Intellectual Property (IP) Rights

Do not post content that uses other people's intellectual property. This includes:  
- Using a public figure's name, image, or likeness in your content
- Featuring brands in your content if you're not promoting their products
- Promoting counterfeit or knockoff products for sale:
**Counterfeits** are unauthorized copies or replicas of actual branded products. They tend to feature the brand's trademarks. These can be a logo, slogan, symbol, design, expression, pattern, or monogram.  
**Knockoffs closely resemble** branded products, but they are not copies or replicas. They might "copy" elements of a branded product's design, feel or look, or some of its features or functions.

## Stay Transparent with Pricing

**Be clear and specific when mentioning prices or promotions in your content.**If an offer only applies to certain customers, purchase amounts (e.g., a minimum order of 3 items or $55), time periods, products, or locations (e.g., residents of Tennessee), make sure to state this explicitly.

## Avoid Misleading Claims

As the holiday season picks up, be mindful of these common misleading content trends, from exaggerated product results to unrealistic claims to keep your videos compliant and trustworthy.  
**Before & After Comparisons** Creators often use before-and-after content (e.g., skin tightening or hair growth) to show product results, but this can mislead audiences if not done carefully. Use disclaimers such as *“Results may vary”* to clarify that outcomes differ for others.  
**Note:** **Disclaimers** are permitted only when showing before-and-after comparisons or referencing time-based results (e.g., “results in X days”).  
**Against Scientific Theory** TikTok Shop prohibits content that promotes product effects that are physiologically impossible or contradict well-established scientific principles. Claims suggesting supernatural influence or control (e.g., “Make someone fall in love with you”) are not allowed. No exemptions apply. Unscientific or impossible claims will be rejected.  
**Exaggerated Claims** Avoid content implying extreme physical or sexual transformations, such as breast enlargement or performance enhancement. Do not promise physical changes within a set time (e.g., “See results in 3 days!”) unless a disclaimer clearly states *“Results may differ from person to person.”* Avoid unrealistic or exaggerated promises altogether.  
**Unrealistic Product Functions** Do not claim that a product performs beyond its physical or mechanical capabilities. For example, flashlights that start fires or toothpaste that “whitens instantly.” Such exaggerated or false claims are prohibited.  
**Digitally Generated Effects** Content must not use **filters, edits, or visual effects** that falsely imply or exaggerate a product’s results. This includes filters that smooth skin, whiten teeth, or reshape body figures. Always show product effects naturally and in real time, without enhancements. No disclaimers or exceptions make digital exaggeration acceptable.

## False Promises

Keep in mind that any content featuring promotions, especially giveaways or purchase-based incentives, must be clear, honest, and not mislead customers with promises of financial rewards.  
**Giveaways** are promotions where customers can win free gifts without making a purchase. Follow these best practices to ensure your content meets platform guidelines.  
- Use TikTok Shop's Live Giveaway Gift Tool to host giveaways.
- Clearly describe how participants can enter (e.g., comment or follow).
- State the number of winners and what they will receive.
- Collect delivery details only after winners are confirmed.
**Purchase-Based Incentives** are promotions where customers receive a free item after buying a product. Keep these tips in mind to make sure your content stays clear, accurate, and policy-aligned.  
- Mention both the main product and free item clearly in your content.
- Ensure the product title and promotion details match (e.g., “Buy one lipstick, get a travel-size mascara free”).
- Avoid exaggerating the value or availability of free items.
