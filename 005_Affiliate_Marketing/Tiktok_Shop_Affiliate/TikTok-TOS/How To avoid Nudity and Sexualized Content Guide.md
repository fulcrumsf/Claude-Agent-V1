## ✅ Best Practices

### Choose professional camera angles and avoid nudity or implied nudity

- Choose professional camera angles that do not emphasize body parts
- Cover more than half of the breast area, and avoid breast close-ups that take up most of the screen.
- Fully cover the intergluteal cleft (butt crack), and avoid buttock close-ups that dominate the majority of the frame.

### Promote Products in a Natural Way

- Focus on product features like fabric, comfort, fit, stretch, and design
- Highlight practical use cases (daily wear, layering, comfort)
- Use a neutral, informative tone and avoid suggestive language

- For intimate hygiene products, use clear, educational language about function and benefits
	- For example, "gentle formulas" or "soothing ingredients"
- For shapewear, focus on comfort, material quality, and everyday wear support
