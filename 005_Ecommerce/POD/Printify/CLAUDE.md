# CLAUDE.md — Printify

One of Tony's two print-on-demand (POD) fulfillment partners (the other is Printful — see `005_Ecommerce/POD/Printful/CLAUDE.md`).

## What it is

Printify is a print-on-demand **marketplace**, not a manufacturer itself — orders route to whichever third-party print provider a seller selects from its network (90+ print providers, 140+ facilities globally). Largest POD catalog by size: 1,300+ products. Free to start, pay only when an order is placed. Integrates with Shopify, Etsy, WooCommerce, eBay, Squarespace, Wix, BigCommerce, PrestaShop, and its own API.

## How Tony uses it

- Fulfillment for **Unomas-Creative's TikTok Shop** (sells via Printify) — currently a couple of sales, including skateboard stickers (a skeleton design).
- Also runs a standalone **Printify store** (separate from the Etsy shops).
- One of the two fulfillment partners referenced for the Bored Nomad Etsy store's POD items (see `005_Ecommerce/Etsy-Bored_Nomad/CLAUDE.md`).
- Tony is considering a future **Shopify store** (possibly Bored Nomad, possibly a new brand) — Printify would be a likely fulfillment option there too.
