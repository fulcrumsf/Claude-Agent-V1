# CLAUDE.md — Printful

One of Tony's two print-on-demand (POD) fulfillment partners (the other is Printify — see `005_Ecommerce/POD/Printify/CLAUDE.md`).

## What it is

Printful is a print-on-demand fulfillment platform that prints, packs, and ships custom products directly in its own facilities (400+ product types: apparel, accessories, home decor, stationery). No monthly fees, no inventory, no upfront cost per unit. **Not a marketplace** — unlike Etsy or Redbubble, Printful doesn't bring customers; it only fulfills orders placed through a seller's own storefront (Shopify, Etsy, WooCommerce, etc.).

## How Tony uses it

- One of the two fulfillment partners for the **Bored Nomad Etsy store** (see `005_Ecommerce/Etsy-Bored_Nomad/CLAUDE.md`) — apparel, art prints, accessories.
- Tony is considering a future **Shopify store** (possibly Bored Nomad, possibly a new brand) — Printful would be a likely fulfillment option there too, given its in-house production/quality consistency.
