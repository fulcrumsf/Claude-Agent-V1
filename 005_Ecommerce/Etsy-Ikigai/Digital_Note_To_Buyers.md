### ✤✤ How to Download Your Files on Etsy&#39;s Website ✤✤  
Follow these steps to download your purchase:  
1. Log in to [Etsy.com](https://www.etsy.com).  
2. Click the **dropdown arrow** at the top of the page.  
3. Select **Purchases and Reviews**.  
4. Click **Download Files** next to your order.  
5. You will receive a PDF with a download link to your purchase files.

Additional Info:
🖼️ If the digital file you purchased is smaller than 20MB, it will be available in the file type shown in the listing (e.g., .jpg, .png, .svg, .pdf).

📂 Etsy limits files to 20MB per file and allows a maximum of 5 files per download. Since Ikigai Digital Studio specializes in high-resolution images, many files exceed this limit. In those cases, you’ll receive a PDF with a direct Google Drive link to download your files seamlessly.


👤 Guest Buyers:
If you are ordering as a Guest, Etsy will email you the download link for your purchase once payment is processed. Sometimes these emails end up in your spam or junk folder, so be sure to check there if you don’t see it in your inbox.


📢 Are you viewing this message from the Etsy Mobile App? 📱❌
Currently, you cannot download digital purchases through the Etsy App. 
1. To access your files, please sign in to Etsy using a web browser on your phone or computer.
2. Click your **Profile Icon** at the top right of the page.  
3. Select **Purchases**.  
4. Click on **Your Order.**
5. Click on **Download Files**
6. Then look for the **Cloud Icon** and click it to download your file.
7. Then select which folder on your mobile device or computer to save to.

  
Need more help? Check out [Etsy’s help article](https://www.etsy.com/help/article/3949) for detailed instructions.
